# 🌴 Time off and team

Your people, the leave they ask for, and who is away when. For a team lead or whoever keeps leave at a small company: each person's days left are worked out, without weekends and holidays.

## Setting it up

- **Kinds of leave:** Annual, Sick, Unpaid and Remote. The first is taken from the yearly allowance; the others are kept but not counted against it.
- **Days of leave a year:** **10 days** to **30 days**; 20, or 14 on a device in Turkey. Each person's can be changed on their screen.
- **Public holidays:** **None**, **Turkey**, **United States**, **United Kingdom** or **Germany**; your device's country when it is one of these, else None. This year's and next year's are added, and leave is counted without them. Add the days you close yourself.
- **Is leave approved by someone?** Yes, unless you say no: a request then waits for **Approve** or **Reject**. Without approvals, leave is approved as it is asked.
- **Days left carried into the next year:** **None**, **Up to 5**, **Up to 10** or **All of them**; up to 5 unless you pick another. **Carry over**, at the end of the year, moves them.

## Who's away

A row for each of your people, a bar for each leave in its kind's color, approved or asked for: a day to a column, the weekend shaded. Press a bar to open the leave.

## Leave

Every leave asked for. The tabs are **To approve**, **Upcoming**, **Away now** and **Past**; the screen sums up how many are to approve, away now and coming. Each row says who, the kind, when, "Nov 2 – Nov 4" or "Nov 4 · morning", the days, and its status.

A leave keeps **Who**, **Type**, **First day**, **Last day**, **One day only**, **Days**, **Status** and a **Note**.

- Its days are its working days, Monday to Friday, less the holidays in it. A morning or an afternoon is half a day. They are worked out as it is saved, and again when its days change.
- A leave that runs into another of the same person, or goes past their days left, is marked. It is kept.
- **Approve** writes who approved it. **Reject** asks why, and writes it in its note. **Cancel** cancels one that is still to come.

## Employees

Your people, with their team, their manager, the days they have left, and a bar of what they used, "5 of 16". A new one asks their **Name**, **Team**, **Manager**, **Email**, **Started** and **Leave a year**.

A person's window says where they stand: "Platform · 11 days left of 16 · 3 requested · next away Nov 2". It lists their leave, and has its buttons:

- **Request leave** asks the kind, the first and the last day, and for a single day **Full day**, **Morning** or **Afternoon**. It says how many days that is, how many are left after it and, with approvals, who it goes to: "3 days · 11 left after this. It goes to Ahmet Koç for approval."
- **Carry over**, at the end of the year, takes what is left into the next year, up to the limit you set. **Carry over all**, on Employees, does it for everyone at once.

## Holidays

The public holidays and the days the company is closed, on a calendar. Leave is counted without them.

## Today

- With approvals, a request comes to Today with **Approve** from the day it was asked until it is approved, with its days and what is left after: "Nov 2 – Nov 4 · 3 days · 11 left after".
- An approved leave is in Today and Upcoming on its days, so you see who is away.

## Home

**Away** shows who is away today and who is coming up. **To approve** lists the requests waiting. **Holidays** shows the ones ahead.

## With other apps

With [Recruiting](recruiting) in the same app, **Hire** adds the person to Employees, with the position's team and the day they start.

## Ask an agent

- "Request annual leave for Ayşe from November 2 to November 4."
- "Who is away next week?"
- "Approve Mert's leave request."
- "How many days of leave does Ayşe have left this year?"
- "Add December 31 to Holidays as a day we close."
