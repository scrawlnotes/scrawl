# 🏠 Home, car and documents

The documents you keep, a passport or a policy, and when each runs out; and what is done again and again at home and for the car, a reminder each. Each comes to Today on its day, and **Done** plans the next time.

## Setting it up

- **What do you keep here?** **Documents**, **Cars** and **Home reminders**, all three by default. With none picked, documents.
- **Your homes**, asked with home reminders: **Home** to start. A home keeps its reminders and documents.
- **Reminders at home:** Boiler service every year, Smoke alarm batteries every year, Air conditioner filters every 3 months and Water purifier filters every 6 months to start. Each is set up for each home.
- **Your cars**, asked with cars: **Car** to start. A name for each: Car, or its make.
- **Reminders for the car:** Inspection every 2 years, Insurance every year, Service every year and Tyre change every 6 months to start. Each is set up for each car.

Say how often in a reminder's own words: every week, month, 3 months, 6 months, year or 2 years, or every 10 days, 5 weeks and the like. One that says none is every year. The reminders come with no **Next** day: give each its day.

## Reminders

What is done again and again: each with its **Home** or **Car**, its **Next** day, how often as **Every**, **Remind** and **Last done**.

- **Every** is **Weekly**, **Monthly**, **Every 3 months**, **Every 6 months**, **Yearly**, the default, **Every 2 years**, or **Custom**, which asks **How many** and **Of**: **Days**, **Weeks**, **Months** or **Years**.
- **Remind** says when it comes to Today: **On the day**, the default, **1 week before** or **1 month before**.
- **Done** writes today as its **Last done**, and its **Next** as often as **Every** says, counted from today.
- One past its **Next** day is marked **Overdue**.

With one home, a reminder saved with no home or car is that home's. With cars and no homes, and one car, a reminder saved with no car is that car's.

## Documents

A manual, a passport, a policy: its **Name**, its **Home** or **Car** if it has one, and the day it **Expires**, when it does. Put its file in its notes. One that has run out is marked **Expired**.

## Cars

Each car's **Name** and **Plate**, and its **Next**: the first of its reminders' days. A car with a reminder past its day is marked: **A reminder is past its day**. Its window lists its reminders and its documents.

## Homes

Each home's **Name** and **Address**. Its window lists its reminders and its documents.

## Today

- A reminder comes to Today on its day, or a week or a month before as **Remind** says, with **Done**, and to Upcoming before. Until you press **Done**, it stays in Overdue.
- A document comes to Today 30 days before it expires, to have time to renew it, and to Upcoming before.
