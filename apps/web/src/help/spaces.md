# 🗂️ Spaces and folders

Everything in Scrawl is in a folder, and every folder is in a space. A space is a set of folders that syncs as one: yours alone, or shared with the people you work with.

## Spaces

Your **personal space** is only yours, and its **Inbox** is where things without a folder go. A **shared space** is one you work on with others: a OneDrive folder shared with them, or a space on a Scrawl server.

With more than one space, each is a heading in the sidebar that folds. Drag a heading to put the spaces in order; your personal space stays first. The settings button beside a heading's **+** has its order, **Settings…**, **Move up** and **Move down**, and **Remove from this device**.

**Settings › Spaces** lists them all. **Add space…** adds one: a OneDrive folder someone shared with you, or a space on a server. Removing a space takes it off this device only; it stays where it syncs and on your other devices.

Today, Upcoming, Search and filters cover every space. Something in another space says it before its folder: `Acme · Sales`.

## Folders

**+** on a space's heading or on a folder makes a note, a list, a board, a keys file, a collection, an app or a folder there. The settings button beside it chooses how the folder is ordered:

- **Group by:** **Type** keeps lists, boards, collections, folders and notes apart; **None** mixes them. **Folders first** keeps folders at the top.
- **Sort by:** **Name**, **Date created** or **Last modified**, ascending or descending.

Each folder keeps its own order, on this device.

## Moving and renaming

Drag a note, a list, a board, a collection or a folder onto a folder, or use **Move to…** in its menu. **Rename** renames it in place. Links to it from other notes keep working.

Moving something to another space copies it there, with its files, and puts the original in its own space's trash.

## Files

**Files**, at the foot of the sidebar, lists every image, recording and file added to a note, a card or a record: where each is used, its size and its day. Find one by its name, or show the largest first.

## Trash

Nothing is deleted at once. **Trash**, at the foot of the sidebar, has what was deleted in every space, each under its space, with where it was. **Restore** puts it back where it was; **Delete forever** and **Empty trash** ask first.

A deleted task, section or card goes back to its list or board, in its place.
