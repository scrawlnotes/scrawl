# 🗓️ Appointments and bookings

Appointments with your customers, or bookings of your rooms and tables: who, when and with whom, on a calendar. For anyone who gives a consultation, a lesson or a haircut, alone or with a team, or lets out a meeting room, a table or a car.

## Setting it up

- **What are the appointments for?** A service, such as a consultation or a lesson, by default; booking a room or a thing; or **Both**. A service brings **Services**, a room or a thing brings **Resources**.
- **Who takes the appointments?** **Just me** by default, or **A team**. For a team, list **Your team**: each gets a calendar of their own, and hours from 09:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday, to change on their screen.
- **Your services:** each as its name, then its minutes: `Consultation 30`. It starts with Consultation 30 and Follow-up 15; one with no minutes takes half an hour.
- **Your rooms and things:** Room 1 and Room 2 to start.
- **Do you charge for them?** Yes by default: services and appointments get a **Price**.
- **Remind them the day before?** Yes by default: Today lists whom to call or message, with **Reminded**.
- **Write a done appointment as a payment?** Asked when the app has Budget and spending and you charge; yes by default.

## Appointments

A calendar of the week. With a team it is colored by whom each is with, and its Day has a row for each person; without one, by how each stands. Each person named when setting up also has a calendar of their own, beside the whole one.

Press **Book** on a customer, or drag over the hours on the calendar. An appointment has its **Customer**, **Service**, **With** whom, **Resource**, **Start**, **Duration**, **Status**, **Price** and **Note**. Left empty, its title is the customer's name and the service, and its length and price come from the service. With rooms and things only, it takes an hour unless you change it.

An appointment is **Booked**, **Confirmed**, **Arrived**, **Done**, **No-show** or **Cancelled**. Its buttons:

- **Confirm** makes a booked one Confirmed.
- **Arrived**, on the day, when they come.
- **Done** when it is over. It asks **Next visit**: **In 1 week**, **In 2 weeks**, **In 4 weeks**, **In 6 weeks** or **Not now**. A choice books the next one at the same time, with the same person, and says whether that time is free. If it is taken, it is booked anyway and marked.
- **No-show** when they did not come, once its time has passed.
- **Reschedule** asks for the new time, and it is Booked again.
- **Cancel** asks why, and writes it in its note.

Nothing is refused, but some things are marked: with a team, an appointment that runs into another of the same person, such as "Overlaps with Elif Kaya · Consultation, 14:30", or falls outside their hours or working days; and a room or a thing booked twice at the same time.

## People

Your customers. **Book** on a customer asks the **Service**, **With** whom and **When**, and says the times free that day: every half hour from 09:00 to 18:30 when nothing else is booked for as long as the service takes, within that person's hours and days. When nothing is free, it says so.

A customer's window lists their appointments day by day, each as "Done · $80". Without CRM in the app, it also says their visits in one line: "9 visits · last Oct 16 · next Nov 13 · 1 no-show".

## Services, Staff and Resources

- **Services:** each with its **Duration** and **Price**, and with a team, who it is **Given by**.
- **Staff:** each person's **Phone**, **Email**, **Works from**, **Works until** and **Days**, how many appointments they have **Today**, and their appointments.
- **Resources:** each room or thing, its **Type**, room, table, vehicle or equipment, its **Capacity**, and its bookings.

## Today

- Each appointment comes to Today on its day, and to Upcoming before it, with **Done**.
- With reminders, a booked one is in Today the day before, as "tomorrow 13:00 · Consultation with Ayşe" with the customer's phone. **Reminded** makes it Confirmed.

## Home

- **Today:** how many are booked today and, when you charge, what they come to.
- **This week:** how many are booked, and how many each person in the team has.
- **No-shows:** who did not come this month.

## With other apps

- [CRM](crm): the same **People**.
- [Budget and spending](budget): with **Write a done appointment as a payment?**, an appointment set to Done adds a payment of its price, under an **Appointments** budget line, and its window lists it.
