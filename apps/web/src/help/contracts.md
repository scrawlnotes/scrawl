# 📜 Contracts and renewals

Leases, insurance, suppliers, software and your customers' contracts: with whom, from when to when, what each costs, and by when to give notice. Before a contract renews by itself, its notice day comes to Today.

## Setting it up

- **Which contracts do you keep?** Customer, Supplier, Lease, Insurance, Software and Service, all picked. They are the choices of a contract's **Type**. With none picked, there is no Type.
- **How much notice do you give, usually?** **None**, **15 days**, **30 days**, **60 days** or **90 days**; 30 days unless you pick another. Each contract can have its own. Its notice day is its end less its notice.
- **Keep the number of seats of software licences?** Asked when you keep Software contracts; yes unless you say no. A software contract then has **Seats**.

## Contracts

The tabs are **Active**, **To decide**, **Ending**, **Drafts** and **Ended**. **To decide** has the contracts whose notice day is in the next 60 days; **Ending**, those that end in the next 60 days and will not renew. The screen sums up how many are active, what they cost a year, how many are to decide and how many are ending.

Each row says whom it is with, its type, its status, what it costs, "$1,200 a month", and its days: "Renews Nov 29 · notice by today", "Ends Mar 31 · doesn't renew", "Sent 5 days ago".

### A contract

A new one asks its **Name**, **With**, **Type**, **Status**, **Start**, **Ends**, **Renews**, **Amount** and **Period**.

- **With** is a company.
- **Renews** is **By itself** or **No**. One that renews by itself also has **For another**, how long each renewal is, from 1 month to 3 years, and **Notice (days)**.
- **Period** is **Monthly**, **Quarterly** or **Yearly**. What it comes to in a year is worked out.
- An end before the start is marked.

Its window says where it stands in one line: "Active · $14,400 a year · renews by itself Nov 29 · notice by today". Put the signed copy in its notes. **History** lists what happened to it: sent, signed, renewed, notice given, ended; a renewal with what it did to the price.

A contract is **Draft**, **Out for signature**, **Active**, **Notice given** or **Ended**. A new one is Active; one still to sign, set to Draft. Its buttons:

- **Send for signature:** a draft is out for signature from today.
- **Signed:** asks the day it was signed, when it starts and when it ends, and makes it Active.
- **Renew:** asks until when and the new amount, and shows the change, "$1,200 → $1,300, +8%".
- **Give notice:** asks the day and how, **Email**, **Letter** or **Portal**. It stays **Notice given** until its end, then ends by itself.
- **End:** ends it now, and asks why.

## Companies

The companies you have contracts with. A company's window lists its contracts, each with its status and its days.

## Today

- For a contract that renews by itself, its notice day comes to Today and Upcoming with **Give notice**. Once the day has gone by, it leaves Today: the contract is left to renew.
- On its end day, an active contract comes with **Renew**. One that renews by itself is renewed by itself that day, for another term at the same price.
- One given notice ends by itself on its end day.

## Home

**To decide** and **Ending** show what is coming in the next 60 days. **A year** shows what the active contracts cost a year, and how much of it is each type.

## With other apps

- **Companies** are shared with the other apps that keep companies, such as [CRM](crm) and [Sales and invoicing](sales).
- With People, from [CRM](crm) or [Customer support](support), a contract has a **Contact**, and a person's window lists their contracts.
- With [Budget and spending](budget), **Signed** also asks the **Budget line**. What the contract costs, or brings for a customer's, becomes a payment that comes again by its period. Given notice or ended, it comes no more. The contract's window lists its payments.
