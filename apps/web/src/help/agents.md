# 🤖 Agents

An agent, such as Claude, works on the same notes, tasks, boards and collections you see here, as you would: it reads them, writes them, moves cards, adds records and presses buttons. You ask in your own words.

## Connecting one

An agent reaches your notes where you can.

- **On a computer:** Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Antigravity work through the Scrawl desktop app, on the spaces on that computer, whatever they sync with. In a browser your notes are out of their reach.
- **On the web and your phone:** Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini reach a space on a Scrawl server. You sign in to the server once, from the agent; it then sees the spaces you are in.

**Settings › Agents** has the steps for each, with what to copy.

## What to ask

- **Turn a meeting into work:** "Add a card to the Launch board for each decision in today's meeting note."
- **Catch up:** "What is due today?" or "What did Ayşe work on since Monday?"
- **Keep records:** "Add Lara Turizm as a customer and log today's call with them."
- **Plan:** "Break the pricing page into tasks in the Website list, due this week."
- **Set up an app:** "Set up a CRM with sales and stock for our spare parts business, in TRY and EUR."
- **Press a button:** "Approve Ayşe's taxi expense." or "Count the Rail stock as 12."
- **Tidy up:** "Move last year's project notes into an Archive folder." Links to them keep working.

To make an app of your own with it, see [Building an app with an agent](apps-building).

## Its own name

Each agent works under a name of its own, such as **Claude Code · Kaan**. Its comments, its moves on a card and the tasks it finishes carry that name, and a card or a task can be given to it. **Settings › Agents** lists the agents that worked in your spaces.

## What it cannot do

- It cannot open keys files, attached files or Scrawl's settings.
- It deletes nothing for good: what it deletes goes to **Trash**.
- When it rewrites a note, the earlier version stays in **History**.

On a computer, what an agent writes while Scrawl is closed waits until Scrawl opens. **Keep running when the window is closed**, in **Settings › General**, keeps it syncing.
