# 🗒️ Quick notes

Quick notes are for a few lines you want to keep: a door code, a gift idea, a phone number. They show as cards with what they say, so you read them without opening them. They are in your personal space only, and in no folder.

## Take one

Open **Quick notes** in **Browse** and press **+** at the bottom right. Write a title if you like, then the note; it is saved when you go back. One with nothing in it leaves nothing behind.

**Quick note** is also the first choice in **+** in Browse and in a folder.

## Pin and order

Press and hold a card for **Pin**, which keeps it at the top under **Pinned**, and for **Move to…**, which makes it an ordinary note in a folder. Hold a card and move your finger to drag it into your own order; **Sort** puts them by the day each was made or last changed instead, and **Custom** goes back to yours.

## Find

The search box over the cards looks through their titles and what they say, and marks the words found. Quick notes are found by **Search** too.
