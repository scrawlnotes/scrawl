# 💬 Chat

Chat is where a team talks, beside its work: a message can become a task or a card, and a note, a task or a card can be put in a message. History is never deleted, and it all stays on your team's own server.

Chat is there when you are in a shared space on a Scrawl server: see [What a server is](server-about). A space synced with OneDrive talks in its cards' comments instead.

## Rooms and direct messages

**Chat** is the fifth tab: your rooms and direct messages in one list, the latest first. **All**, **Unread**, **Rooms** and **Direct** over it narrow the list.

- A **room** belongs to a space. A public one is open to everyone in the space, to read and to **Join**; a private one only to its members, who add others. Every space has a **general** room.
- A **direct message** is between you and up to eight others who share a space with you.

**+** in Chat's heading makes a room or a message, or browses the rooms. A conversation's **Info** has its people, its files and its settings.

## Messages

Write in Markdown, and add photos, videos, files and voice recordings: hold the microphone to record. Press and hold a message to react, **Reply**, **Forward**, edit or delete your own, or make it a task or a card. Swipe a message to the right to reply to it.

Write `@` and a name to mention someone, or `@room` for everyone in a room.

## Notifications

A direct message and a mention always notify you; a private room every message, a public room only mentions. Each conversation's bell chooses **All**, **Mentions** or **Off**. Turn notifications on for this phone in **Settings › Notifications**. On an iPhone, they come only to Scrawl on the home screen.

## Search

The search box in Chat looks through every conversation. The **Search** tab finds messages too, under **Messages**.

## Agents

An agent connected through the server reads the conversations you can, and writes in them as itself. See [Agents](agents).
