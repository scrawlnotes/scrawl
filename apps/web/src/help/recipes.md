# 🥗 Recipes and meal plan

Your recipes and where each is written, and a plan to eat by, such as a diet. The plan's days are written for you: on each, you only tick what you ate. With a shopping list, a recipe writes what to buy.

## Setting it up

- **Your meals:** the meals of a day, in their order: **Breakfast**, **Snack**, **Lunch** and **Dinner** to start, up to eight. A plan's items are written meal by meal.
- **Keep a shopping list?** Yes by default: it adds **Shopping list**, and **Add to shopping list** on a recipe.

## Days

Each day of your plans, written as a plan is saved, with what to eat at each meal. It opens on **From today**; **All** has the days gone by too, and **Calendar** shows them by the day. Each day says its **Status**, how many of its meals were eaten, as "2 of 4", and how many were **Changed**.

Open a day:

- Its items are under each meal. The items of one meal are alternatives, with "or" between them.
- Tick **Eaten** on the one you ate, or write what you had in its place in **Instead**.
- Each meal says "2 to choose from" until then, and "eaten" or "changed" after. Over them, the day: "2 of 4 meals · 1 changed".
- **Didn't follow** marks the whole day. A day marked so with meals ticked is marked: **Didn't follow, but meals are ticked**.

Nothing has to be filled in: a day you leave as it is stays **Followed**.

## Plans

A plan to eat by: its **Days** of the week, all of them to start, **From**, today to start, and **Until**, four weeks after **From** to start. Put the dietitian's list in its notes.

Its window lists its items under each meal, the items of a meal its alternatives. An item is what to eat, as you would say it, in **What**, with its **Meal**, the days it is **On**, **Every day** or one weekday, and a **Recipe** if it has one. An item with a recipe and no **What** takes the recipe's name. A plan says how many **Items** it has, and on how many days you **Didn't follow** it.

- Saving a plan writes each of its days from **From** to **Until**, on its **Days**, each with the items of its weekday.
- Made longer, it gets the new days; made shorter, it loses the days no one touched.
- A change to its items reaches its days from today on that no one touched: none ticked, none written in, not marked **Didn't follow**. The others, and the days gone by, stay as they were.

## Recipes

Each recipe's **Link**, where it is written, its **Type** (**Main**, **Soup**, **Salad**, **Side**, **Dessert**, **Breakfast** or **Snack**), its **Time**, how many it **Serves**, and its **Tags**. How it is made goes in its notes.

Its window lists its **Ingredients**, each with **How much**. A recipe with none is marked **No ingredients yet**.

## Shopping list

What to buy: each **Item**, **How much**, the recipe it is **For**, and **Bought**. It opens on **To buy**, what is not bought yet.

**Add to shopping list**, on a recipe, writes each of its ingredients on the list, with how much.

## Today

Today's day comes to Today, not to Upcoming before it. Open it there to tick what you ate.
