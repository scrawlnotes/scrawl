# 🚨 Incidents and actions

Outages, quality and safety issues, complaints that grew, security breaches: what happened, how bad it is, what is done while it lasts, why it happened, and what is done so that it does not happen again. For a team that runs a service, a site or a shop floor.

## Setting it up

- **Kinds of incident:** Outage, Quality, Safety, Complaint and Security. They are the choices of an incident's **Type**. With none, there is no Type.
- **Keep what is to be done on a board?** Yes, unless you say no. It adds the Actions board, and **Add action** puts a card on it, with who and by when.
- **When is its review written?** Two, five or ten days after it is resolved; five unless you pick another. The review comes to its owner's Today with **Write review**.

## Incidents

Add an incident as soon as it is noticed: **What happened**, its **Type** and its **Severity**, **Critical**, **Major** or **Minor**. It starts now, as **Investigating**, with you as its owner, and its timeline begins with "Reported". Put photos and files in its notes.

The tabs are **Open**, **To review**, **Closed** and **All**. The screen sums up how many are open, how many are critical, how many reviews are due and how many actions are open. Each row says its type, severity and status, how long it has lasted, "2 h 15 min so far" or "Resolved in 3 h 20 min · review due", its owner and its open actions.

### An incident

Its window shows its status, **Investigating**, **Fixing**, **Monitoring**, **Resolved** or **Closed**, and a line such as "Fixing · Critical · 2 h 15 min so far". Its **Timeline** has every update, with who posted it and when. Its buttons:

- **Post update** asks the status and **What's happening**, and writes it on the timeline. **Resolved** also writes when it ended.
- **Write review**, once it is resolved, asks the **Root cause**, the **Impact** and **What we learned**.
- **Add action**, with the Actions board, asks what is to be done, who and by when, and puts a card on Actions in To do, linked to the incident.
- **Close** closes it once its review is written; before that it says **Write its review first**. One closed without its review is marked.

## Actions

What is done so that it does not happen again, a card each, in **To do**, **Doing**, **Done** or **Won't do**. A card shows its incident, and an incident's window shows its cards. See [Boards](boards).

## Today

- An incident not resolved is in Today every day with **Post update**.
- Once it is resolved, its review comes to its owner's Today after the days you chose, with **Write review**, and stays until it is written. It shows in Upcoming before.
- An action's card comes to Today on its day.

## Home

**Open now** shows how many are open, how many are critical, and how long each has lasted. **This month** counts the month's incidents by severity, and the reviews due.

## With other apps

With [Customer support](support), a request can point at the incident it is part of. An incident's window lists its requests, and its line counts them. When the incident is resolved, its requests open again, their reply due now.

## Ask an agent

- "Report an incident: checkout is down, Outage, Critical."
- "Post an update on INC-0012: fixing, the payment provider's keys were rotated."
- "Write the review for INC-0012 and add an action for Mert to add an alert on key expiry by Friday."
- "Which incidents still need a review?"
