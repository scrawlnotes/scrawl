# 🤝 CRM

CRM keeps your customers, the people you talk to there, every call and meeting, and the deals on their way. It is for a team that sells: after each talk you write what was said and plan what comes next, and it comes back to you on its day.

## Setting it up

- **Who are your customers?** **Companies and the people there**, the default, or **People only**. With people only there is no Companies: activities and deals belong to a person.
- **Will you track deals?** Yes by default: a **Deals** board is set up, and the app keeps money in the currencies you choose. With no, there are no deals.
- **The stages of a deal** are the columns of Deals, in this order: **Lead**, **Contacted**, **Meeting**, **Proposal**, **Won** and **Lost** to start. Rename, add or take out any. **Won** closes a deal as done, **Lost** as dropped; the list says which stage is **waiting**, which **done** and which **dropped**.

The app's **About** note says what each stage means; for a stage of your own, write it there.

## Deals

Each deal is a card on the stage it has reached. Its card shows its company and contact, its value, and its next step: the first activity planned for it, green, red when it is late, and **No next step** when there is none. Each column adds up the value of its deals, and the board says how many have no next step.

A deal keeps its **Company**, **Contact**, **Value**, **Source** (**Referral**, **Website**, **Event**, **Outbound**, **Partner**), the day it should close by as **Expected close**, and its **Owner**. With more than one currency, it has its **Currency**.

Open a deal, and its window has its stages as a bar, and:

- **Log** and **Plan**, as on its company, with the deal already picked. They need the deal's company, or its contact when your customers are people: pick it first.
- **Next steps**, what is planned for it, and **History**, what happened, day by day.
- **Won** and **Lost**.

A deal moved to **Lost** asks **Lost because**: **Price**, **Timing**, **Competitor**, **No budget**, **No response** or **Not a fit**. A deal moved to **Won** writes **Customer** in its company's **Type**.

## Companies and people

A company keeps its **Name**, **Website**, **Phone**, **Email**, **City**, **Size**, **Industry**, **Type** (**Customer**, **Supplier**, **Partner**) and **Owner**, you to start with. A person keeps their **Name**, **Company**, **Job title**, **Email** and **Phone**; a person picks their company as you type it.

Each says where it stands, worked out by itself: "Customer · İstanbul · 3 people · last contact 3 days ago", "Project Manager · Acme Lojistik · last contact 3 days ago". A company is a **Customer** once it has won a deal or its **Type** says so, a **Prospect** while it has a deal open, else a **Lead**; **Supplier** and **Partner** as its **Type** says. **Last contact** is the day of the latest activity done with it.

Their windows have:

- **Log**, after a call, a meeting, an email or a note: its **Type**, **When**, now to start, on a company **With** whom, the **Deal**, and **What was said**. Under **Next step**, what comes next: its type, what and when. It writes the activity, and the next step as a planned one; a next step with no **When** is for tomorrow.
- **Plan**, for a call, a meeting, an email or a task to come, with its day.
- **Planned**, what is to come, and **History**, what happened, day by day.
- A company's people and deals, a person's deals, and what other apps link to them.

In Companies, the **Customers** tab lists your customers, and **Gone quiet** those with no contact for more than 30 days, or none.

## Activities

Every call, meeting, email, note and task: its **Type**, **When**, the company, person and deal it is about, **What was said**, and **By**, you to start with. It is **Done** once it happened, planned until then.

The **To do** tab lists what is planned under **Overdue**, **Today** and **Planned**, each with **Done**. The **Done** tab lists what happened, the latest first.

- An activity with no name is named by its type and whom it is with: "Call with Ayşe Kaya".
- One with a person and no company takes the person's company.
- One with no company and no person is marked: **Link it to a company or a person**. With people only, it needs the person.
- **Done** asks what was said and the next step, as **Log** does.

## Today

An activity planned comes to Today on its day with **Done**, and to Upcoming before it. **Done** asks what was said and what comes next, so each call plans the next one. One not done stays in Overdue until it is.

## Home

- **Today**: what is planned for today.
- **Pipeline**: each open stage, how many deals are in it and what they add up to.
- **Closing this month**: the deals whose **Expected close** is this month, with their value.
- **Gone quiet**: the customers with no contact for more than 30 days, and how long it has been for each.

## With other apps

CRM's companies and people are the same ones the other business apps use. Each adds what it keeps to their windows: orders, requests, contracts, projects, purchase orders.

- [Sales and invoicing](sales): a deal's window lists its quotes, and **Quote** makes one with its company. **Accept** on a quote moves the deal to **Won**, **Declined** to **Lost**; a deal moved to **Won** confirms its quote, one moved to **Lost** declines it. A company says what is left to collect from it.
- [Customer support](support): a company's and a person's window list their requests, and **Request** adds one.
- [Product development](product): **Ship** on a release can plan an email to each company that asked for what went out, in Today.
