# 🧮 Shared expenses

Who paid what, on a trip or at home, who owes whom, and settling up. Add each expense with who paid it; each one's share and balance are worked out, and **Settle up** says who pays whom.

Keep the app in a shared space, and each one adds what they paid; else one keeps it for all. See [Working together](working-together).

## Setting it up

- **Who do you share with?** Yourself too. They are the people of the first group, **Home**, or of each new trip with [Travel](travel); add more any time.
- **How are expenses split?** **Equally**, the default, or **Equally, by amounts or by parts**.
- **Write your own share in Personal finance?** Asked when the app has [Personal finance](finance); yes by default.

The app keeps money in the currencies you choose as it is set up.

## Expenses

Add each expense: its **Title**, **Amount**, **Paid by**, **When**, today to start, and its **Group**. **Paid by** starts as whoever paid last, and the group as the last expense's. With more than one currency in the app, an expense has its currency beside the amount, and in another one its **Rate**: the rate used last for that currency. Attach the receipt to it.

Its window lists each one of the group, with a tick and their share, worked out as you type. All of the group share it: untick who is not in it. The one who paid says **paid**, and the cents left over are theirs.

With **Equally, by amounts or by parts**, its **For whom** says how:

- **Equally**, the default.
- **By amounts**: type each one's share beside them. Until they come to the total, it says how much is missing.
- **By parts**: each one has 1x to start; − and + change it, and their share is the total over the parts.

The shares are split equally again when the amount, who paid or who is in it changes. An expense whose shares do not come to its total is marked: "The shares come to 90, not 100".

Tick **Every month** on the rent. Its **Next** day, a month after it to start, is the day it is written again, with its shares, and **Next** moves a month on. **Add again** writes it now.

The list opens on **All**: the expenses by group, the latest first, with who paid and how much.

## Groups

Each group whose expenses are shared: a flat, a family. **Home** is ready, with the people you named. Each row says how many people it has, what it spent this month and who still pays whom, as "Can → Ayşe $40".

Its window says "3 people · $1240 this month", and has:

- **People**, each with their balance: what they paid less their share. Above nothing they are owed; below it they owe, and it is marked: "Owes 40". Add a person here.
- **Who pays whom**, each with its amount, ticked **Paid** as it is paid.
- **Settle up**: after you confirm, it writes who pays whom how much, so that nobody owes anything: at most one payment fewer than there are people. The payments not ticked yet are taken back first.
- Its expenses, the latest first, with who paid and how much.

A payment ticked **Paid** gets its **Paid on** day and counts in the balances; untick it, and it is taken back.

A person's window has what they **Paid**, their **Share** and their **Balance**, and each of their shares: what it **Comes to** and what they paid of it.

## Today

An expense ticked **Every month** comes to Upcoming before its **Next** day, with **Add again**. On the day it is written again by itself, and **Undo** takes it back.

## Home

- **Today:** what comes today.
- **Groups:** what each group spent this month, and who still pays whom.

## With other apps

- [Travel](travel): a trip is the group, and **Trips** takes the place of **Groups**. A new trip takes the people the app was set up with; add others in its window. Its window has **People**, **Who pays whom** and **Settle up**. A booking with a cost is an expense paid by the one in its **Paid by**, shared equally. There is no **Every month** on a trip.
- [Personal finance](finance): tick **Me** on yourself among the people. Your own share of each expense is written as spent in **Transactions**, from your bank account, and is written again when the expense changes. The rent written again each month writes your share again too.
