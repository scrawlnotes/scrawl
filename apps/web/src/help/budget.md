# 💰 Budget and spending

What you plan to earn and spend each month against what was paid, the bills to pay and the money to collect, in one place. With expenses, what your people spent too, approved and paid back.

## Setting it up

- **Which categories do you split your spending into?** **Rent**, **Salaries**, **Marketing**, **Software**, **Travel** and **Other** to start with. A budget line and a payment each have one.
- **Do you follow payments by their due day?** Yes by default: a payment has its **Due** day, and one past it that is not paid shows as overdue. With no, payments go by their **Date**.
- **Will you track your people's expenses too?** No by default. Yes adds **Expenses** and asks **The categories of an expense**: **Travel**, **Meals**, **Supplies**, **Equipment** and **Other** to start with.
- **Add the payment to collect when an order is invoiced?** Asked with [Sales and invoicing](sales), when payments go by their due day. Yes by default.

## Budget

A budget line is one kind of income or spending: its **Type**, **Income** or **Expense**, its **Category**, and what it is **Planned a month**.

**This month** and **This year** show the lines under **Income** and **Expense**, each with what was planned, what was paid, a **Used** bar and what is **Left**. A year plans twelve months. An expense line's bar turns yellow at 80% of its plan and red over it. Over the lines: what came in and what went out and, this month, the net and how many lines are over budget.

A line's window lists its payments, with their total.

## Bills and payments

Every payment in and out: its **Direction**, **In** or **Out**, its **Amount**, **To or from**, **Budget line**, **Status** (**Planned**, **Invoiced** or **Paid**), **Due** day and **Method**.

The tabs are **To pay**, **Overdue**, **To collect** and **Paid**. Over them: what is to pay this week, what is overdue and what is to collect. Each row says its day: "Overdue 4 days", "Due today", "Due Nov 3".

- A payment out needs its budget line: "Pick the budget line". Given one, it takes the line's category.
- **Comes again**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly** or **Yearly**, is for rent, salaries, software. Once it is paid, the next one is added, due a period later.
- **Pays itself**, for a direct debit or a standing order, marks one that comes again paid on its day.
- **Mark paid** asks the **Amount**, the day it was **Paid on** and **How**: **Bank transfer**, **Card** or **Cash**. Paid in part, the rest stays to pay as a payment of its own.

## Expenses

What your people spent and are to be paid back for. An expense has **What**, **Who**, its **Day**, **Amount** and **Category**, with a photo of the receipt attached. It starts **Submitted**.

The tabs are **Mine**, **To approve**, **To pay back**, **Paid back** and **Rejected**. Over them: how many wait for approval and how much, and how much is to pay back. Each row on **To approve** has **Approve**.

- **Approve** asks its **Budget line**, saying where the line would stand this month with it. It writes who approved, and adds what is to pay back to **To pay**. Once that payment is paid, the expense is **Reimbursed**.
- **Reject** asks **Why**.

## Today

A payment not yet paid comes to Today on its due day with **Mark paid**, saying to or from whom and how much, and to Upcoming before. One that **Pays itself** is marked paid by itself on its day. Late ones stay under **Overdue**.

## Home

- **Over budget:** the lines over their plan, each with what is left.
- **This month:** the net, what came in and went out, and the budget line most spent on.
- **To pay:** what is due this week and what is overdue, each with its day.
- **To approve**, with expenses: how many wait, and how much.

## With other apps

**Bills and payments** is where an app keeps all its money; other templates write into it.

- [Sales and invoicing](sales): a **Sales** income line, which an order's payments go on. With the question above, an order marked **Invoiced** adds the payment to collect, with its total and due day; when an order is cancelled, its unpaid payments are taken away.
- [Project management](projects): a budget line, a payment and an expense can be given a project, and the project adds up its budget lines and what was paid for it.
- [Stock and purchasing](stock): a purchase order received adds what you owe the supplier.
- [Assets and maintenance](assets): a repair or service with a cost goes on a **Maintenance** line.
- [Subscriptions and memberships](subscriptions), [Contracts and renewals](contracts), [Appointments and bookings](appointments), [Field service](field), [Consulting](consulting) and [E-commerce](ecommerce) can write what comes in or goes out as payments too.
