# 💻 Scrawl on your devices

Scrawl is the same app on the web, on a computer and on a phone. Each keeps its own copy of your spaces; with sync, each shows the same work. See [Sync](sync).

## Web

Open [app.scrawlnotes.com](https://app.scrawlnotes.com) in any browser. Once it has loaded, it works offline too.

What you write is kept in that browser. Another browser, or a private window, starts empty: nothing is lost, it is still in the first one.

## Desktop, for Windows and Mac

On the web, press **Downloads** at the foot of the sidebar and install it. Your notes are files in a folder on your computer.

- When a new version is out, the sidebar says so; it comes when you choose, and **Restart to update** puts it in place.
- With **Keep running when the window is closed**, in **Settings › General**, Scrawl stays in the tray, or the menu bar on a Mac, and keeps syncing. **Open at login** starts it with your computer.
- Agents on your computer, such as Claude Code or Claude Desktop, need the desktop app: in a browser your notes are out of their reach. See [Agents](agents).

## Phone, for iPhone and Android

Scrawl is not in the app stores. Open app.scrawlnotes.com on your phone and add it to the home screen: in Safari use **Share › Add to Home Screen**, in Chrome use **⋮ › Add to Home screen**. It then opens from the home screen like any app, with a layout made for the phone:

- **Recent**, **Browse**, **Today** and **Search** at the foot of the screen, and **Chat** with a Scrawl server.
- Everything opens as a page; back, or a swipe from the left edge, goes back.
- **+** at the bottom right adds what the page is for: a task, a card, a record, a note.
- Press and hold a row for its menu.

On an iPhone, notifications come only to Scrawl on the home screen.
