# 🗂️ Spaces and folders

Everything in Scrawl is in a folder, and every folder is in a space. A space is a set of folders that syncs as one: yours alone, or shared with the people you work with.

## Spaces

Your **personal space** is only yours, and its **Inbox** is where things without a folder go. A **shared space** is one you work on with others: a OneDrive folder shared with them, or a space on a Scrawl server.

With more than one space, each is a section of **Browse** with its folders under it.

**Settings › Spaces** lists them all; **Reorder** puts the shared ones in order. **Add space…** adds one: a OneDrive folder someone shared with you, or a space on a server. Removing a space takes it off this phone only; it stays where it syncs and on your other devices.

Today, Upcoming, Search and filters cover every space. Something in another space says it before its folder: `Acme · Sales`.

## Folders

In **Browse** or in a folder, **+** at the bottom right makes a note, a list, a board, a keys file, a collection, an app or a folder there. A folder's **···** menu chooses how it is ordered: **Group by** and **Sort by**.

## Moving and renaming

Press and hold a row for its menu: **Move to…** moves it to another folder, **Rename** renames it. Links to it from other notes keep working.

Moving something to another space copies it there, with its files, and puts the original in its own space's trash.

## Files

**Files**, in **Browse**, lists every image, recording and file added to a note, a card or a record: where each is used, its size and its day.

## Trash

Nothing is deleted at once. **Trash**, in **Browse**, has what was deleted in every space, with where it was. Tap one to **Restore** it where it was, or to delete it for good.

A deleted task, section or card goes back to its list or board, in its place.
