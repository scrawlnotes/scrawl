# 🎂 People and birthdays

The people close to you, their birthdays every year, and keeping in touch. A birthday comes to Today on its day, and so does someone you have not talked to for too long.

## Setting it up

- **Keep anniversaries too?** Yes by default: each person has an **Anniversary** too, and it comes as their birthday does.
- **How often to keep in touch with someone, to start with:** **Every week**, **Every 2 weeks**, **Every month**, the default, **Every 2 months**, **Every 3 months** or **No reminders**. Each one has their own; this is what a new person starts with.

## People

A person keeps their name, **Relation** (**Family**, **Friend**, **Partner**, **Colleague**, **Other**), **Birthday**, **Anniversary**, **Phone**, **Email** and **Keep in touch**, from **Every week** to **Every year**. A birthday needs only its day and month: give the year if you know it, and it says the age they turn. A new one asks only their name, relation, birthday, phone and how often to keep in touch. Write in their notes what you wish to remember: their family, what they like, a gift idea.

Its window says where you stand, "Friend · birthday on 12 Nov · last talked 33 days ago", and has **Talked** and **Wished**. It lists the moments you talked, day by day, the latest first, each with its sign, 📞 🏠 💬, and its note.

- **Birthdays** opens first: everyone with a birthday or an anniversary, by the next one, under **Today**, **Tomorrow** and each month, "Nov 2026". Each says "Turns 36 · 29 Oct", "12 Nov" when the year is not known, or "9th anniversary · 20 Nov", and how far off it is: "in 14 days".
- **Keep in touch** lists everyone given one, the first due first, with how late or how long is left, "3 days late", "today", "4 weeks left", and when you last talked.

### Moments

**Talked** writes a moment today. Add one yourself to say how and what about: its **When**, **Type**, **Call**, **Visit** or **Message**, and **Note**. It is named by itself: "Call with Can Demir". **Last talked** is the day of the latest one.

## Today

- A birthday or an anniversary comes to Upcoming before it and to Today on its day, as "Turns 36 · Birthday" or "9th anniversary", with **Wished**. It writes a message moment, "Birthday wishes", and moves the day to next year. **Wished** works only on their day.
- Counted from the last time you talked, someone you have not talked to for longer than their **Keep in touch** comes to Today with **Talked**; late, they stay in Overdue until you press it. Someone you have never talked to does not come: their window says "Not talked yet".

## Home

- **Today:** the birthdays and the people to talk to today, with **Wished** and **Talked**.
- **People:** the next birthdays and anniversaries, each with how far off it is.
