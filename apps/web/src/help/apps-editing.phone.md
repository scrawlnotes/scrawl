# ✏️ Editing an app

An app is yours once it is set up. Change its answers, change its parts, or tell an agent what you want different. Its records are never deleted along the way.

## Changing its answers

Press and hold the app's row in **Browse** and choose **Edit app…**. It goes through the app's setup again with today's answers:

1. **App:** its name, its folder and its currencies. A new name renames its folder and its About note too; links follow.
2. A page for each template, with its questions. A changed answer shows what it was before; a stage taken off a list can be put back.
3. **Changes:** what will be added, what changes, what is taken off and what is kept, before anything is written.

**Save** writes it all at once, and **Undo** takes it all back.

What an answer brings is added: a board, a field, a button, a view. What the old answer brought is taken off, but only its settings: rules, buttons and views. Boards, collections, fields and records stay as they are, and Changes says so: "Deals board stays; trash it if you don't need it". A rule or a view you changed yourself is kept too.

## Changing its parts

Everything an app has is ordinary Scrawl. A collection's form and rules are designed in **Fields** on a computer; a board's columns and phases, a view's sorting and grouping, and the About note are changed where they are. See [Collections](collections) and [Boards](boards).

## With an agent

An agent connected to Scrawl changes an app from what you tell it. For example:

- "Add a Quote stage to the CRM's deals, before Proposal."
- "Track each set in Gym, not only each move."
- "Add a Delivery date field to orders, and show late ones in red."
- "When a support request is closed, set its closing day."
- "Add a button to projects that adds our standard first tasks."

It answers with what it changed. What it rewrites keeps its earlier version in **History**, what it deletes goes to **Trash**, and its changes carry its name. See [Agents](agents).
