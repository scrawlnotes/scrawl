# 🔁 Subscriptions and memberships

Plans, who is on them and when each renews; what comes in each month. For software sold by the month or the year, a gym's or a club's memberships, or service agreements.

## Setting it up

- **What do you sell this way?** **Subscriptions** by default, **Memberships** or **Service agreements**. Only the names change: with Memberships they are memberships, and the one who pays is a **Member**.
- **Your plans:** each renews as its name says, monthly, quarterly or yearly; monthly when it says none. It starts with Basic monthly, Pro monthly and Pro yearly. Write each price on its plan.
- **Is there a free trial?** No by default. With yes, a new one starts as a trial and renews when the trial ends; each plan has its **Trial days**, 14 to start.
- **Who pays you?** **Companies** by default, or **People**. Asked when the app has no CRM.
- **Write each renewal as a payment?** Asked with Budget and spending and without Sales and invoicing; yes by default.

## Subscriptions

Its tabs are **Active**, which has the non-renewing ones too, **Trial** when there is a trial, **Past due**, **Paused** and **Cancelled**. Over them: the MRR, how many are active, how many renew this week and how many are past due. Each row says its plan, its status, its price as "$49 / month", and its day: "Renews Mar 3", "Past due 5 days", "Ends Nov 30".

A new one asks its customer, its **Plan**, the day it **Started**, and whether it **Renews by itself**: tick it when it is paid by card or direct debit. Its **Amount** and **Period** come from the plan, its name from the customer, and **Renews on** is a period after it started, or the end of its trial. Its MRR is its amount spread over the months of its period.

One is **Trial**, **Active**, **Non-renewing**, **Paused** or **Cancelled**. An active one whose renewal day has passed is past due.

Its window says it in one line, "Active · $588 a year ($49 MRR) · renews Mar 3 · customer since Jan 2025", and its **History** keeps what happened to it, each with what it changed of the MRR.

Its buttons:

- **Renew** when a period is paid: **Renews on** moves a period on.
- **Convert** makes a trial a paying one.
- **Change plan** asks the **New plan**, and **From**: **Now** or **At renewal**. It says the new price and how the MRR changes. At renewal, the plan is kept as **Plan at renewal** until **Renew** puts it in place.
- **Pause** asks **Until** when; left empty, until you resume it. **Resume** makes it active again.
- **Cancel subscription** asks **When**, **Now** or **At end of term**, and **Why**: **Too expensive**, **Not using it**, **Missing feature**, **Switched**, **Closed** or **Other**. At the end of its term, it stays **Non-renewing** until its renewal day, and **End** then makes it Cancelled. A trial or a paused one is cancelled at once.
- **Reactivate** brings back a cancelled or non-renewing one.

With Memberships the button is **Cancel membership**, with Service agreements **Cancel service agreement**.

## Plans

Each plan's **Period**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly** or **Yearly**, its **Price**, how many are **Active** on it and their **MRR**, and who is on it.

A customer's window shows their active subscriptions and their MRR, and lists them, with **+ Subscription**, or **+ Membership**.

## Today

- A renewal comes to Today on its day, and to Upcoming before it, with **Renew**. One that renews by itself is renewed once its day has come, and **Undo** takes it back.
- A trial ending comes with **Convert**.
- A pause ending comes with **Resume**.

## Home

- **MRR:** the MRR of the active ones, with what came new and what was cancelled this month, and what is past due.
- **Renewals:** what renews this week.
- **Churn:** how many cancelled, the reason given most, and how many are past due.

## With other apps

- [CRM](crm): the same customers.
- [Sales and invoicing](sales): when Sales keeps the same customers, **Renew** adds an order to invoice with the plan, and the window lists its invoices.
- [Budget and spending](budget): without Sales, **Renew** writes a payment of the renewal, under a budget line named after the list, such as Subscriptions, and the window lists its payments.
