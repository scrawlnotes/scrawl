# 🧩 Collections

A collection keeps records of one kind, each on a form of its own: companies, people, candidates, payments, anything you keep a list of. You design the form, so it holds what you need and nothing else.

A collection is a folder. Each record in it is a Markdown file: its fields at the top, its notes below.

## Making one

In **Browse** or in a folder, press **+** at the bottom right and pick **Collection**. Start from a template, **Companies**, **People**, **Activities**, **Feedback** or **Payments**, or from an empty one.

For a set of collections that work together, a CRM or a stock list, start from an app instead: see [How apps work](apps-how).

## Records and the list

On a phone a collection is a list: each record's name with two or three of its fields. **+** adds a record. Search and the views are at the top; the **View** sheet sorts, groups and chooses the fields the list shows.

A record is a page: its name, buttons to call, write or open its site, its fields line by line, and its notes. What links to it, such as a company's people, is in tabs beside **Details**. Text is typed in place; a choice, a link or a date is picked in a sheet.

## Designing the form

The form is designed with **Fields**, on a computer or by an agent. Its fields can be **Text**, **Number**, **Date**, **Time**, **Select**, **Multi-select**, **Checkbox**, **URL**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Person**, **Link**, and the record's **Name** and **Code**.

A **Link** field points to a record of another collection: a person's company, a payment's project. **Formula**, **Lookup** and **Rollup** work out a value: `budget - spent`, the city of a person's company, the total of a project's payments.

## Rules and buttons

A collection can check a wrong value and say why, have buttons on a record that do several things at once, such as **Start project**, and do something when a record is saved. An agent writes them for you: see [Building an app with an agent](apps-building).

## A calendar

A collection with dates can have a calendar view, by the day, the week or the month. Its records come to Today and Upcoming on their day too.

## Records in your work

A card's link field, or a task's **Record**, points to a record. A task given a record shows in Today with the record's name.
