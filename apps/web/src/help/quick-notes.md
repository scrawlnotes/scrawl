# 🗒️ Quick notes

Quick notes are for a few lines you want to keep: a door code, a gift idea, a phone number. They show as cards with what they say, so you read them without opening them. They are in your personal space only, and in no folder.

## Take one

Open **Quick notes** in the sidebar, under Filters & Labels, and click **Take a note…**. Write a title if you like, then the note. It is saved when you close it or click outside it; one with nothing in it leaves nothing behind.

**Quick note** is also the first choice in **+**: it opens an empty one over what you are doing.

## Pin and order

Pin a note to keep it at the top, under **Pinned**. Drag the cards to put them in your own order; **Sort** puts them by the day each was made or last changed instead, and **Custom** goes back to yours.

A card's menu has **Move to…**, which makes it an ordinary note in a folder.

## Find

The search box over the cards looks through their titles and what they say, and marks the words found. Quick notes are found by **Search** too.
