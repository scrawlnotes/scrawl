# 🩺 Health

What you need to remember about your health, or your family's, with as little to type as can be: when a medicine runs out, your appointments, the check-ups and vaccines that come again, and your records.

## Setting it up

- **Who is it for?** **Just me** by default, or **My family**. With a family, list **Who is in your family**: each record has **For**, whose it is, picked from them; with no one listed, written in.
- **Keep medicines?** Yes by default: it adds **Medicines**.
- **Check-ups and vaccines that come again:** Dentist every 6 months, General check-up every year, Eye exam every 2 years and Flu shot every year to start. Say how often in its own words: every 6 months, year or 2 years, or every 10 years and the like; every year when it says none. With a family, each is set up for each person.

The check-ups come with no **Next** day: give each its day.

## Medicines

Each medicine's **Dose**, **How often** (**Once a day**, the default, **Twice a day**, **3 times a day** or **As needed**), how many come **In a box**, and **Since** when, today to start.

It works out when it **Runs out**: **Since**, and as many days as the box lasts at that dose. **Taking** lists them with what is left: "5 days left", "Runs out today", "Ran out". One taken as needed, or with nothing in **In a box**, has no day.

- **Refilled**, with a new box: it counts from today.
- **Stopped**, when you no longer take it: it leaves **Taking**.
- One that runs out within 5 days is marked **Runs out soon**.

## Appointments

An appointment is its **Doctor**, **When**, its day and time, and **Where**. The doctors and places written before are offered as you type.

**Book follow-up**, after it, asks **When**: **In 2 weeks**, **In 1 month**, **In 3 months**, **In 6 months**, the default, or **In 1 year**, from its day. It books the next one with the same doctor, place and person, on that day with no time yet.

## Check-ups

Check-ups and vaccines that come again: each with how often as **Every**, its **Next** day and **Last done**.

- **Every** is **Weekly**, **Monthly**, **Every 3 months**, **Every 6 months**, **Yearly**, the default, **Every 2 years**, or **Custom**, which asks **How many** and **Of**: **Days**, **Weeks**, **Months** or **Years**.
- **Done** writes today as its **Last done**, and its **Next** as often as **Every** says, counted from today.
- One past its **Next** day is marked **Overdue**.

## Records

A lab result, a report, a prescription: its **Name**, its **Kind** (**Lab result**, **Report**, **Prescription**, **Imaging** or **Other**) and its **Date**, today to start. Put its file in its notes.

## Today

- A medicine you are taking comes to Today 5 days before it runs out, saying how many days are left, with **Refilled**, and to Upcoming before. Until it is refilled, it stays in Overdue.
- An appointment comes to Today on its day, saying where, and to Upcoming before.
- A check-up comes to Today on its **Next** day with **Done**, and to Upcoming before. Until it is done, it stays in Overdue.

With a family, each row says whose it is.
