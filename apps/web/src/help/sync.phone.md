# 🔄 Sync

Scrawl has no cloud of its own: what you write is saved on this phone. Sync keeps the same spaces on your other devices, and with the people you share them with, through OneDrive or a Scrawl server.

## How it works

Every device keeps a whole copy of each space and works on it, online or not. Sync sends what changed here and brings what changed elsewhere; offline, it waits and sends it later.

- Two devices that changed the same note are put together line by line. When they cannot be, the other version is kept as a copy beside it.
- A task, a card or a record changed in two places keeps the changes of both, field by field.
- What sync takes away goes to the trash first.

**Sync**, in **Browse**, says how it stands, and opens a page with every space, what it did and what it could not. When the phone is offline, or something has not been sent, a strip over the tabs says so on every page.

## OneDrive

Your own Microsoft storage. Good for your own devices, and for a folder shared with others.

1. Open **Sync** in **Browse**, then **Sync…**, and choose **OneDrive**.
2. Sign in with your Microsoft account.
3. Choose the folder the space goes in, or make a new one.

## Scrawl server

A server your team runs. A change reaches the others in a second or two, and the server adds accounts, invites and Chat. Connect it in **Settings › Spaces › Add space… › Connect a server…** with its address or an invite link. See [What a server is](server-about).

## Your other devices

On your computer or another device, open Scrawl, press **Sync**, and choose the same OneDrive folder or the same server. Your shared spaces and servers come along with your personal space: once it has synced, they are listed there too.

## Sharing a OneDrive folder

Share the space's folder with someone in OneDrive. They add it in **Settings › Spaces › Add space…**, from what was shared with them, and it is a space on their devices too. What they may change is what OneDrive lets them.

## Large files

Notes, lists and boards always come down. A file over 10 MB waits in the cloud until you open it; its place shows its name and size with **Download**.

## When something is wrong

The Sync page says which space cannot sync and why: signed out, offline, its folder gone. Each says what to do, such as **Sign in**.

A space synced by a newer Scrawl stops on an older one, which says **Update Scrawl to keep syncing**: open Scrawl again to update, and it goes on.
