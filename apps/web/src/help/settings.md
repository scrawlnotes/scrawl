# ⚙️ Settings

**Settings**, at the foot of the sidebar, opens in a window, its sections on the left. What you choose is used at once.

## Profile

Your name and your email. Your name goes on what you do where others see it: a comment, a move on a card, a task given to you. The email says which person you are in a shared space; nothing is sent to it.

## Appearance

- **Mode:** **System** follows your device, or always **Light** or **Dark**.
- **Theme:** the colors of the whole app, each shown as it looks.
- **Size:** how large everything is, one for a phone and one for a computer.

Each device keeps its own.

## General

On the desktop app: **Keep running when the window is closed**, which keeps syncing in the tray, and **Open at login**. See [Scrawl on your devices](devices).

## Notifications

With a Scrawl server: whether messages, mentions and the rest show as notifications on this device, and a test one. Under it, **Notify me about** picks what the server tells you of, **Mentions**, **Assignments**, **Reminders** and **Due dates**, the same on every device of yours.

## Calendars

**Connect Google Calendar** signs in with a Google account, and **Connect Outlook Calendar** with a work or a personal Microsoft account, apart from OneDrive; you can connect one of each. Their meetings then show together in Today, Upcoming and the calendar; a click opens one in Google Calendar or Outlook, where it is changed. **Show events in Scrawl** turns them all off, and the box beside each calendar picks which show: Google's start as you ticked them in Google Calendar; in Outlook yours start on, one shared with you off. What you pick is the same on every device; each device connects on its own.

## Spaces

Your personal space, the spaces you share with others, the servers you are connected to, and this device. A space's page has its name, where it is kept on this device, where it syncs, and its people. **Add space…** adds a shared OneDrive folder or a server's space. See [Spaces and folders](spaces) and [Sync](sync).

## Agents

How to connect each agent, on this computer and through your servers, and the agents that have worked in your spaces. See [Agents](ai-agents). **Answer in Scrawl**, under a server, sets up Claude, ChatGPT or another agent to answer when you mention it. See [Agents that answer in Scrawl](agents-answer).

## Logs

What Scrawl did on this device, such as opening and syncing, with its warnings and errors. **Copy** and **Save…** give them to send us when something goes wrong. They leave this device only then.

## About

The version, what is new in it, and its updates.
