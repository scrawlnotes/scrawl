# 🏷️ Assets and maintenance

What the company owns: who has it and until when, where it is, what it is worth today, its warranty, its repairs and its service. For a company's computers, phones, furniture, vehicles and machines.

## Setting it up

- **Your asset categories:** **Computers**, **Phones**, **Furniture**, **Vehicles**, **Machines** and **Other** to start with.
- **Do you give assets to people?** Yes by default: an asset has who it is **With** and when it is **Due back**, with **Check out** and **Check in**.
- **Do you service them regularly?** Yes by default: each asset says how often; when a service is done, the next is planned.
- **Do you have more than one place?** No by default. Yes asks **Your places**, **Head office** and **Warehouse** to start with. With [Stock and purchasing](stock), it is the same list as its warehouses.
- **Add the cost of maintenance to Payments?** Asked with [Budget and spending](budget). Yes by default.

## Assets

An asset has its **Code**, **Category**, **Serial no**, **Supplier**, the day it was **Bought**, its **Cost**, **Warranty until**, how long it **Lasts (years)**, and how often it is **Serviced**: **Monthly**, **Every 3 months**, **Every 6 months** or **Yearly**. Its **Status** is **In use**, **Available**, **In repair** or **Retired**.

Worked out by itself:

- **Value:** what it is worth today, falling evenly from its cost over the years it lasts. Left empty, the years are its category's: 3 for computers and phones, 5 for vehicles, 7 for furniture, 10 for machines.
- **Next service:** from the day it was bought, or today, as often as it is serviced.
- **Repairs and service:** what its repairs and services done cost.

The tabs are **In use**, **Available**, **In repair**, **Due back**, **With me** and **Retired**. Over them: how many assets, what they are worth, how many are late back, and how many warranties end within 30 days. A row says who has it or where it is, and "Due back Nov 20", or "since Mar 4" for one in repair.

Its window starts with a line such as "In use · Ayşe Kaya since Mar 4 · due back Nov 20 · $1,240 now ($1,800 bought) · under warranty". Beside its fields is its **History**: each check out and in, repair and service, written by its buttons.

- **Check out**, on an available one, asks **To** whom, or with places **Or a place**, the day it is **Due back**, and a **Note**. Left without a day, it has none to come back.
- **Check in** asks **How it came back**: **Good**, **Damaged** or **Needs repair**, which sends it to **In repair**. With places, it asks where it is **Put in**.
- **Send to repair** asks **What is wrong**. **Back from repair** asks its **Cost** and **What was done**, and makes it **Available** again.
- **Service done** asks its **Cost** and **What was done**, and plans the next service.
- **Retire**, when it is sold, thrown away or lost, asks first. A retired asset is with nobody.

## Maintenance

Every line of every asset's history. It opens on **Repairs and service**: the repairs, services and inspections, with their asset, day, status and cost.

An entry has its **Asset**, its **Type** (**Checked out**, **Checked in**, **Repair**, **Service** or **Inspection**), its **Day**, its **Status**, **Planned** or **Done**, who it was **Done by**, its **Cost** and a **Note**.

## Locations

With places, each place with its **Address** and the assets in it.

## Today

- The day an asset is due back comes to Today with **Check in**, saying who has it, and to Upcoming before.
- A service due comes the same way, with **Service done**.
- The day a warranty ends comes to Upcoming, and to Today on the day.

An asset late back or a service late stays under **Overdue** until its button is pressed.

## Home

- **Value:** what the assets are worth now and what they cost, the category **Most of it** is in, and what went on **Repairs and service**.
- **Due back:** what is out with a day to come back, and how many are late.
- **Coming up:** the next services; with no regular service, the warranties ending.

## With other apps

- [Stock and purchasing](stock): with more than one warehouse there, your places are the same **Locations**; a place of the same name is added once.
- [Budget and spending](budget): a **Maintenance** expense line. A repair or service done with a cost adds what is owed to **Bills and payments**, **Invoiced**, on that line; set back from **Done**, its unpaid payment is taken away.
- With an app that keeps companies, such as [CRM](crm) or [Stock and purchasing](stock), the **Supplier** is one of its companies, and a company's window lists the assets from it.
