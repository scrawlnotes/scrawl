# 📚 Reading and watching

What you read, watch, listen to and play: where to find each, how far you are, what is next and what you thought. Each kind has a screen of its own, in its own words: you are **Watching** an anime and **Reading** a book.

## Setting it up

- **What do you keep?** **Books**, **Films**, **Series**, **Anime**, **Manga**, **Webtoons**, **Podcasts** and **Games**, all of them to start. Each one you keep is a screen.
- **Where do you watch and read?** The places a work can be: Netflix, Max, Disney+, Prime Video, Apple TV+, Hulu, YouTube, Crunchyroll, Webtoon, Kindle and Paper to start; in Turkey, Exxen, TOD, Gain and Mubi in place of Apple TV+ and Hulu. Add or take out any. They are the choices of **Where**; with none, there is no **Where**.
- **A goal for the year:** **None** to start, or 12, 24, 36 or 52. It is this year's **Goal** in **Years**.
- **Keep quotes?** Yes by default: **Quotes** keeps the lines you like.
- **Keep what you lend?** No by default. With yes, a book or a manga has **Lent to**, **Lent on** and **Back by**.
- **Keep what comes out when?** Yes by default: a work has **Comes out**, the day a book, a film or a new season is out.

## A screen for each kind

**Books**, **Films**, **Series**, **Anime**, **Manga**, **Webtoons**, **Podcasts** and **Games** each list the works of their kind. Add one on its kind's screen: its title is enough, and it is of that kind. A new one also asks how many pages, episodes or chapters it has, **Where**, its **Status**, the days a new episode comes out and who recommended it, as its kind has them.

A work starts as one you plan to. Its statuses, in its kind's words:

- Books, manga and webtoons: **Plan to read**, **Reading**, **On hold**, **Completed**, **Dropped**.
- Series and anime: **Plan to watch**, **Watching**, **On hold**, **Completed**, **Dropped**.
- Podcasts: **Plan to listen**, **Listening**, **On hold**, **Completed**, **Dropped**.
- Games: **Wishlist**, **Playing**, **On hold**, **Played**, **Dropped**.
- Films: only **To watch** and **Watched**.

The screen groups them by status: what is going on first, then what you plan to and what is on hold, then finished and dropped, those two closed. Each row has its cover, when **Cover** has its link, and under its title who made it and how long, or its year and where: "Andy Weir · 476 pages". Then how far you are, with a bar, "E6 / 12", "p. 212 / 476", "S2 · E6 / 10", "Ch. 254 / 374 · Vol. 22 / 42"; its **Score**; and a **Note**: "New episode today", "11 to go", "Every Sunday", "Ayşe recommended". A film shows where it is and who recommended it, a game its platform.

A work you go through a part at a time has a button on its row and in its window:

- **+1** on a series, anime, manga, webtoon or podcast: one more episode or chapter, written in its log. It sets the work going; the last one finishes it.
- **Update** on a book asks the **Page** you are at, and writes the pages read since in its log. At the last page it is finished.

A work keeps its **Type**, **Status**, who made it (**Author**, **Director**, **Created by**, **Studio**, **Host** or **Developer**, by its kind), **Year**, where you are (**Page**, **Episode** or **Chapter**) of how many (**Pages**, **Episodes** or **Chapters**), a series' **Season**, a manga's **Volume** and **Volumes**, a film's **Runtime** in minutes, a game's **Platform** and **Hours to beat**. Every kind has a **Score** from 1 to 5, **Genres**, ready for each kind, **Where** and **Its link**, **Info** (IMDb, Goodreads, MyAnimeList or another page), **Cover**, **Recommended by** and its **Lists**. Write what you thought in its notes.

A series, anime, manga, webtoon or podcast still coming out has **New episode every**, **New chapter every** for manga and webtoons: the days of the week a new one is out. While you are going through it, it comes to Today on those days. Left empty, it does not.

Its window says it in a line under its title: "Anime · 2023 · 28 episodes · Crunchyroll · watching since 2 Oct". It also has how far you are with its button, its **Log**, day by day, and its quotes.

Worked out by itself:

- Set it going, and **Started** is today; finish it, and **Finished** is today, and it counts in that year, in **Years**.
- Where you are never goes past how many it has.
- A score that is not from 1 to 5 is marked.

## Lists

Your own lists: for the summer, award nominees, what a friend picked. A work's **Lists** puts it on as many as you like. A list counts its **Works**, and its window lists them with their kind and status.

## Quotes

The lines you keep: the **Quote**, **From** which work, its **Page or chapter**, and **Tags**. A work's window lists its quotes.

## Years

Each year: how many you **Finished** in it, your **Goal**, and **At this pace**, how many you will have finished by its end. **Pace** says **On track** or **Behind**. It adds up the **Pages** and **Episodes** you got through, and your **Days in a row**: the days one after another you read or watched something. Its window lists what you finished that year, with its score.

This year is there from the start; the next one is added when you finish something in it.

## Today

- A series, anime, manga, webtoon or podcast you are going through comes to Today on its days, as "E7 out" or "Ch. 161 out", with where it is. **Watched**, or **Read** for a chapter, writes it in the log and moves you on by one; the last one finishes it. A day missed is gone, never piled up, and it is not in Upcoming.
- A work with **Comes out** comes to Upcoming before its day and to Today on it.
- With what you lend, a book or a manga comes to Upcoming before its **Back by** and to Today on it. One not back by then is marked, with who has it since when. When it is back, empty **Lent to**.

## Home

- A box for each kind: how many you are going through, "3 watching", and the first ones with how far you are.
- **Years**: "14 of 24 finished · on track".
