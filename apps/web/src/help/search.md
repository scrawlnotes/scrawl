# 🔍 Search

Search finds anything in every space by its name or its words: a note, a task, a card, a record, a file, a folder or a space.

## Finding

Click **Search** at the top of the sidebar and type. The best match comes first, whatever it is. Each result says where it is, its name, and what it is; one found by its words shows the line they are in.

The tabs under the box narrow it to **Notes**, **Tasks**, **Cards**, **Records** or **Files**; **All** has them in one list. With a Scrawl server, **Messages** finds what was written in Chat too.

## How words match

A word matches from its start: `tu` finds Tulin, not Status. Capitals and accents do not matter. A card or a record is found by the values of its fields, not by their names.

## Opening

A note opens at the words found, marked. A task is shown in its list, a card and a record open in their window, and a folder or a space opens in the sidebar.

Before you type, **Recent** under the box has the things you opened last, the newest first, without the one on screen: **Ctrl+K** and **Enter** go back to the one before. Under it are the searches you opened something from, on this device only; click one to search it again.

## Actions

Under the results are things to do by their name: **New note**, **Create…**, **Today**, **Upcoming**, **Files**, **Trash**, **Settings**, **Help**, **Sync now**, and **Welcome to Scrawl** to open that note again.
