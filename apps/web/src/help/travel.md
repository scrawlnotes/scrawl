# ✈️ Travel

Your trips: where to and when, the bookings day by day, the places to see, what to pack from your own list, and what each trip costs. A trip comes to Today on its first day, and each booking at its time.

## Setting it up

- **What do you keep for a trip?** **Places to see**, **What to pack** and **What it costs**, all three by default. Without places to see, a trip has no places. Without what to pack, there is no **Kind of trip**, no packing and no **Packing list**. Without what it costs, there are no expenses, unless the app has [Shared expenses](split).

The app keeps money in the currencies you choose as it is set up.

## Trips

Trips opens on **Coming**: the trips not over yet, and those with no days, the soonest first. Each row says when, "12–16 Nov · 5 days · in 14 days", what is left of its budget, "$1600 left" or "$200 over", and what is still to pack, "40 to pack".

A trip keeps **Where to**, its **First day** and **Last day**, its **Kind of trip** (**Abroad**, **Flying**, **Beach**, **Cold**, **Work**) and its **Budget**. The days can wait until they are known. Left empty, its name is where to and its month: "Rome · Nov 2026". A last day before the first is marked.

Its window has its days under its name, "12–16 Nov · 5 days · in 14 days", or **No days yet**, and:

- Its money in one line: "$300 spent · $1700 left · $340 a day", what is left for each day to come. A trip over its budget is marked: "Over its budget by 200".
- **To do**, what is to be done before the trip: a visa, the insurance. **Add step** adds one; one given a day comes to Today.
- Its bookings, day by day.
- **Places to visit**, numbered, each with its weekday.
- Its packing, in its groups.
- Its expenses, the latest first.

### Bookings

Add each booking in the trip's window. Its **Type** is **Flight**, **Hotel**, **Car**, **Train** or **Event**, and what it asks follows it:

- A flight: **Flight no**, **Seat**, **From**, **To**, **Departs** and **Arrives**. A train the same, with no flight number. Both are named from and to: "IST → FCO".
- A hotel: its name, **Address**, **Check in** and **Check out**.
- A car: its name, **Address**, **Pick up** and **Drop off**.
- An event: its name, **Address**, **Starts** and **Ends**.

Each has its **Confirmation** and its **Cost**; attach the ticket to it. One that ends before it starts is marked.

In the trip's window each booking is at its time, with its sign, ✈ 🏨 🚗 🚆 🎟, its name, and a line: a flight's number, when it arrives, its confirmation and seat; a hotel's address and confirmation. A hotel shows on its first day as **check in** and again on its last as **check out**; a car as **pick up** and **drop off**.

A booking with a **Cost** writes it as an expense of the trip, and changes it as the cost changes. Take the cost out, and the expense goes too.

### Places

Each place to see has its name, its **Day**, left empty for any day of the trip, a **Link**, and **Been there**.

### Packing

A new trip takes from **Packing list** what is taken on every trip and on its kinds. Tick another kind later, and what it needs comes too; nothing comes twice. Each thing is ticked **Packed** as it goes in the bag, under its group, and the trip says how many are still to pack.

## Expenses

What you spend on the way: its **Title**, **Amount** and **When**, today to start. Its **Trip** is the one you are on, else the one of your last expense. Attach the receipt to it.

With more than one currency in the app, an expense has its currency beside the amount, and in another one its **Rate**: the rate used last for that currency. The list shows each one's day, trip and total, in the main currency.

A trip adds up its expenses, its bookings' costs among them, and from its **Budget** works out what is left, and what is left for each of its days to come.

## Packing list

Your own list of what you take, ready with 62 things in **Documents**, **Money**, **Electronics**, **Clothes**, **Toiletries**, **Health** and **Other**. Each says what it is **Taken on**: **Every trip**, or **Abroad**, **Flying**, **Beach**, **Cold** or **Work**. A passport goes abroad, a swimsuit to a beach, a phone charger everywhere. Add what you always take and take out what you never do.

## Today

- A trip comes to Upcoming before its first day and to Today on it, with what is still to pack.
- Each booking comes on its day, at its time, with its line.
- A step in a trip's **To do** given a day comes on that day.

## Home

- **Today:** what comes today.
- **Trips:** the next trip, large: how far off it is, "in 14 days", "tomorrow" or "now", with its days, what is still to pack and what is left of its budget.

## With other apps

- [Shared expenses](split): a trip is a group whose expenses are shared. A new trip takes the people the app was set up with; add others in its window. Its window has **People**, each with their balance, **Who pays whom**, and **Settle up**. A booking has **Paid by**, one of the trip's people, and its cost is shared equally between them; one with a cost and no one in **Paid by** is marked: "Who paid it?". Its expenses are those of Shared expenses, with who paid and for whom.
