# 🎧 Customer support

Customer support keeps every request a customer sends, the conversation on it, and when its reply is due; and, if you like, what customers ask for. It is for a team that answers customers: the mail itself is sent and read in your mail program, and what was said is kept here.

## Setting it up

- **Where do requests come from?** Any of **Email**, **Phone**, **Chat**, **Web form** and **Social media**; the first three to start. They are the choices of a request's **Channel**.
- **How soon do you answer a request?** **Within 4 hours**, **Within 8 hours**, **Within a working day**, the default, or **Within two working days**. A request's reply is due that long after the customer writes; a working day skips the weekend.
- **Will you collect feedback from customers too?** Yes by default: **Feedback** keeps the ideas customers ask for, and a request can point at its **Idea**.
- **Set up a folder of saved replies?** Yes by default: **Saved replies**, with three to start.

## Requests

**Requests** has a tab for whose they are and where they stand: **Mine**, **Unassigned**, **Waiting on customer** and **Solved**. The list says how many are open, unassigned and late: "12 open · 3 unassigned · 2 late". Each request shows its subject, channel, whom it is from, its status, its **Assignee** and its reply: "Reply due in 3 h", "2 h late", "waiting 2 days". The one due first comes first.

A new request asks the **Customer**, the **Channel**, its **Type** (**Question**, **Problem**, **Request**) and **What they wrote**, which becomes the first message of its conversation. It gets a number such as RQ-0001, a **Priority** (**Low**, **Normal**, **High**, **Urgent**) and a **Status**: **New**, **Open**, **Waiting on customer**, **Solved** or **Closed**. A customer's window lists their requests, and **Request** adds one for them.

Its window says where it stands, "Open · Acme Lojistik · reply 2 h late · asked 23 h ago", and holds its **Conversation** day by day: what the customer wrote, what your team wrote, and your notes. Its buttons:

- **Reply**: write the reply, or pick one in **Saved reply**, then say how it stands after it: **Keep open**, **Waiting on customer** or **Solved**. Send it from your mail; it is kept here, and the reply is no longer due. A request that is no one's becomes yours.
- **Customer wrote**, when they write again: what they wrote. The request opens again, its reply due anew.
- **Note**, for what only your team sees.
- **Assign to me**, on a request that is no one's.

Worked out by itself:

- A new request's reply is due as you answered when setting it up, counted from when the customer wrote.
- A request is late when it is **New** or **Open** and its reply is past due.
- While it is **Waiting on customer**, it says how long it has waited, and no reply is due.

## Feedback

The ideas customers ask for: each with its **Title**, the person and company it came from, its **Source** (**email**, **call**, **interview**, **social**, **support**), **Topic** and **Status** (**new**, **planned**, **done**, **declined**). When a request asks for something new, pick its **Idea** on the request, or add one. Each idea counts the requests that ask for it, "3 requests", lists the customers who asked, and shows those requests in its window.

## Saved replies

A folder of notes, each an answer to something customers often ask. It starts with **We are on it**, **We need a few more details** and **Closing this request**. Each note in it is offered in **Saved reply** when you press **Reply**; add your own as notes in the folder.

## Today

A request's reply comes to Today on the day it is due, with **Reply**, and to Upcoming before it; one late stays in Overdue. Only **New** and **Open** requests come: one waiting on the customer does not.

## Home

- **Requests**: how many are open and how many late, each with its reply.
- **Waiting on customer**: what waits for a customer, and for how long.
- **Most asked**: the ideas the most requests ask for.

## With other apps

- [CRM](crm): the companies and people are the same. A request has a **Company**, the customer's when left empty, and shows the company's other requests; a company's window lists its requests too.
- [Product development](product): the ideas are the same **Feedback**, and a roadmap card says how many requests asked for it. With its **Bugs** board, **Report bug** on a request asks a **Title** and **Severity** and adds a bug card linked to it; the request says "Bug reported". When the bug moves to **Fixed**, the request opens again, its reply due now, saying "Bug fixed".
- [Field service](field): a request lists its jobs, **Job** adds one, and it can name its **Equipment**.
- [Incidents and actions](incidents): a request can point at its **Incident**; when the incident is resolved, its requests open again for their reply.
- [E-commerce](ecommerce): **marketplace questions** and **reviews** are channels too.
