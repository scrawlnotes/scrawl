# 🔥 Habits and goals

What you do again and again, ticked off in Today, and your streaks. And, if you like, your own goals, by a number or by their steps, each saying whether it is on track.

## Setting it up

- **Your habits:** each with how often in its words: "Gym 3 times a week", "Call mom on Sunday", "Yoga on Mon, Wed and Fri"; one that says none is every day. The habit takes its name without them: Gym, Call mom. It starts with Walk 30 minutes, Read 20 pages, Gym 3 times a week, Call mom on Sunday and In bed by 23:00; change, add or take out any.
- **Keep your own goals too?** Yes by default: **Goals**.

## Habits

A habit has its name, **How often**, and **Since**, today to start. How often is **Every day**, **On some days**, picked in **On**, or **Times a week**, 3 to start.

Its row has its last seven days as boxes, ticked on the days you did it. Press a box to tick a day you forgot, or to take one off. Beside them:

- **Streak**: "🔥 12 days"; in weeks for one done a number of times a week.
- **This week**: "6 of 7".
- **30 days**: how much of the last 30 days you kept it.

Its window has the month, to tick days in too, with its **Streak**, its **Best**, and how many times **In 30 days**.

## Goals

A goal's **Measure** is a **Number** or **Steps**:

- By a number, it has its **Unit**, **Start**, 0 to start, **Target** and where it is **Now**: "7 of 24 books". **Update** asks where it is now.
- By steps, it has its **Steps**, each added with **Add step**, a day for each if you like. It moves as they are ticked.

Each has **Since**, today to start, and **By**, the day to reach it. **Progress** shows as a bar, "7 of 24 books" or "2 of 5 steps". **Status** says **On track**; **Behind** when it is short of where it should be by now, between **Since** and **By**; or **Done** when it is reached. One past its **By** and not done is marked: **Past its day**.

**Open** lists the goals not done, the nearest **By** first. **Calendar** shows them on their **By**.

## Today

- A habit comes to Today on its days with **Done**, and **Undo** takes it back. It says your streak, "🔥 5 days", or what is left of the week: "3 of 7 this week", "2 more this week". One done a number of times a week comes each day until the week has as many.
- A day missed is gone, never piled up. Habits are not in Upcoming.
- A goal's step with a day comes to Today on it, and to Upcoming before it, to be ticked there.
- A goal comes to Upcoming before its **By**, and to Today on it.

## Ask an agent

- "Add a habit: stretch on Mon, Wed and Fri."
- "I forgot to tick Read 20 pages yesterday: mark it done."
- "Which habits did I keep least in the last 30 days?"
- "Add a goal: read 24 books by December 31."
- "Update my weight goal: I'm at 78 kg now."
