# 💼 Consulting

Your clients and projects: hours and timers, budgets, expenses, deliverables, and invoicing. For a consultancy, an agency or a freelancer; an accounting or law firm can keep its clients' returns and filings too.

## Setting it up

- **How are you paid?** **A fixed fee**, **By the hour** and **A retainer**, all three by default. A project's **Fee** is then **Fixed fee**, **Hourly** or **Retainer**, or **Non-billable**. A fixed fee brings **Fee payments**.
- **Do you keep your hours?** Yes by default: time entries, timers and the **Time** screen.
- **Are there deadlines that come again and again?** No by default. With yes, list **Your deadlines**, each as often as its name says: monthly, quarterly or yearly, monthly when it says none. It starts with VAT return monthly and Annual accounts yearly.
- **Are your clients companies or people?** Asked when the app has no CRM; companies by default.
- **Write each bill as a payment?** Asked with Budget and spending and without Sales and invoicing; yes by default.

## Projects

Its tabs are **Active** and **Done**, the projects grouped by client, each with its **Fee**, **Budget**, **Spent**, **Used** as a bar, **Remaining** and **Uninvoiced**. Over them: how many are active, what is uninvoiced and how many are over budget.

A project has its **Client**, **Fee**, **Rate** for an hour of work, **Budget**, **Start**, **End**, **Status**, **Active**, **On hold** or **Done**, and **Owner**. Leave **Budget** empty and no budget is followed; a retainer's budget is a month's.

A project adds up its hours, what is spent of its budget, what is left and what is not invoiced; of a retainer, what is spent this month. At 80% its bar turns yellow; over budget it turns red, and the project is marked. Its window says it in one line, "Globex · 48 h · $6,200 of $8,000 · $1,800 left (22%) · $3,400 uninvoiced", and lists its deliverables, hours, expenses, fee payments and invoices.

Its buttons:

- **Track time** asks **What**, **Hours** and **Day**. Leave the hours empty and a timer starts; **Stop** writes them.
- **Expense** asks the **Category**, **Travel**, **Meals**, **Lodging**, **Supplies** or **Other**, then the **Amount**, **Day** and **What**.
- **Invoice**, when something is not invoiced, asks **Hours from**: **This month**, **Last month** or **All not invoiced**; **Expenses**: **Billable ones** or **None**; and, with Sales, **Lines**: **By person**, **By task** or **One line**. What it takes is marked **Invoiced**.

With Project management in the same app, the projects here are called engagements.

## Time

**Week** is a sheet: a row for each project, a column for each day, the hours typed in the cells. **+ Add row** adds a project, and **Copy last week** brings last week's projects. **All** lists every entry.

A time entry has **What**, its project, **Person**, **Day**, **Hours**, **Billable**, **Rate**, **Amount** and **Invoiced**. It takes the rate of its project, and is billable unless the project is non-billable. Left without a name, it is Work. One added with no hours starts a timer.

## Expenses

What was spent on a project: **What**, its project, **Day**, **Category**, **Amount**, **Billable** and **Invoiced**. Attach the receipt to its notes. It is billable unless its project is non-billable; untick **Billable** for what the client does not pay.

## Deliverables

What is delivered for each project, by whom and by when: its **Owner**, **Due** and **Status**, **To do**, **In progress** or **Delivered**. A late one says how many days late it is. **Delivered** marks it done.

## Fee payments

On a fixed-fee project, its payments, each with its **Amount** and **Due** day, added with **+ Payment**. **Invoice** on one marks it **Invoiced**.

## Deadlines

What is due for each client again and again: a return, a filing or a report. A deadline has its **Client**, **Kind**, **Due**, how often it **Repeats**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly** or **Yearly**, and **Status**, **To do** or **Done**. Its title and how often it comes are taken from its kind. When it is set Done, the next one is added a month, a quarter or a year later. A client's window lists their deadlines.

## Today

- A running timer, with **Stop**.
- A deliverable on its due day, and in Upcoming before it, with **Delivered**.
- A fee payment on its due day, and in Upcoming before it, with **Invoice**.
- A deadline on its day, and in Upcoming before it.

## Home

- **Uninvoiced:** what is not invoiced, project by project.
- **Budgets:** how many are over, and how many are at 80% or more.
- **This week:** the hours and how much of them is billable, and which project and who had the most.
