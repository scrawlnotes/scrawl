# ✅ Task lists

A task list is what you have to do, in a folder beside that work's notes. A folder can have as many lists as you need. Your personal space's **Inbox** has one that is always there.

## Tasks

In **Browse** or in a folder, press **+** at the bottom right and pick **Task list**. In the list, **+** adds a task. A task has a title, a description, a date, a priority from 1 to 4, labels, and in a space shared with others who it is given to.

Tap a task for its sheet, where everything about it is changed. In a task's description, `@` mentions someone in the space; with a Scrawl server they are told. See [Working together](working-together).

- **Subtasks:** hold a task and move it to the right, under another, to make it a subtask, up to four levels.
- **Moving:** hold a task and drag it where it should go. **Move to…** in its menu moves it to another list or section.
- **Done:** tap its circle. **Task completed** and **Undo** show for a few seconds.
- **Deleting:** it goes to the trash, with **Undo**; from the trash it goes back to its place.

## Sections

**Add section** is in the list's **···** menu. A section folds; tap its name to rename it, and its **···** menu has the rest.

## Today and Upcoming

The **Today** tab gathers what is due today or late from every list, board and collection, in every space. Tasks added in Today go to the Inbox.

- Late ones are under **Overdue**. **Reschedule** gives them all a new day at once.
- What you finished today is at the end, and opens again with a tap on its circle.

**Upcoming**, beside Today, shows what is coming, day by day, as a list or a calendar.

## Labels

Labels are words you put on tasks and cards, each in a color, such as `bug` or `waiting`. A space's tasks and cards share its labels. Type `@` in a task's title to pick one, or press **Labels** in its sheet.

## Filtering

The filter button at the top of a list narrows it by labels, priority, date and who it is given to.

**Filters & Labels**, in **Browse**, keeps filters across every list and board: ready ones such as **Priority 1**, **Given to me** and **No date**, and your own. Each label has its page there too.

## Favorites

**Add to favorites**, in a list's **···** menu, puts it under **Favorites** at the top of Browse. Notes, boards, collections, keys files, filters and labels are starred the same way.

## With notes and records

A task made with **To task** from a note links back to it. **Record** links a task to a record of a collection, such as a customer: the task shows with the record's name in Today.
