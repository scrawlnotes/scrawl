# ✍️ Content calendar

Content calendar keeps what you will publish, on which channel and when, and the steps before it goes out. It is for a team that publishes articles, posts, videos or newsletters on a plan: each piece's steps come to Today on their days, and review and publishing are a button each.

## Setting it up

- **Where do you publish?** Any of **Blog**, **Newsletter**, **LinkedIn**, **X**, **Instagram**, **YouTube** and **Podcast**; the first three to start. They are the choices of a piece's **Channel**, each with its color on the calendar. With more than one, a piece can be **Also on** others the same day.
- **The steps of each type, before it goes out**: a row for each, its **Step**, **Type** and **Days before**. To start:
  - **Article**: Outline 7, Draft 5, Images 3, Review 2.
  - **Post**: Draft 3, Images 2, Review 1.
  - **Video**: Script 10, Shoot 6, Edit 3, Review 2.
  - **Newsletter**: Draft 3, Review 1.

  A new piece gets its type's steps, each so many days before its day; moving its day moves them.
- **Do you work in campaigns?** No by default. With yes, **Campaigns**, on a timeline.

## Calendar

The app opens on the calendar: each piece on the day it goes out, in its channel's color. It says how many go out this week and how many are in review: "1 going out this week · 1 in review".

Its tabs are **Calendar**, **Ideas**, **In progress**, **Scheduled**, **Published** and **Mine**. The lists say how many pieces are at each stage, and each piece shows its status, channels, the day it goes out, its steps done and who it is by.

## Content

A piece has its **Title**, **Channel**, **Type** (**Article**, **Post**, **Video**, **Newsletter**), the day and time it **Goes out**, **By**, you to start with, and its **Reviewer**. Its **Status** goes **Idea**, **Writing**, **Review**, **Scheduled**, **Published**.

Its window says where it stands, "Review · LinkedIn, Instagram · goes out Fri, Nov 6 (in 7 days) · 3 of 4 steps", and lists its **Steps**, each with its day: tick **Done** as each is done. Its buttons:

- **Send for review**, on an idea or a piece being written: asks the **Reviewer**, and it goes to **Review**.
- **Approve**, on a piece in review: it is **Scheduled**.
- **Request changes**, on a piece in review: asks **What to change**; it goes back to **Writing**, and the piece keeps what to change until it is sent again.
- **Publish**: asks its **Link**, where it is out, and **Out on**, today to start. It is **Published**.

## Campaigns

A campaign has its **Name**, **Goal**, **Owner**, **Start**, **End** and **Status** (**Planned**, **Running**, **Done**). **Campaigns** opens on a timeline, each campaign from its start to its end with its pieces on their days; the tabs **Running**, **Planned** and **Done** list them. Each says how many of its pieces are out: "2 of 5 out".

**Plan content** on a campaign asks a piece's **Title**, **Channel**, **Type** and the day it **Goes out on**, and adds it to the campaign in **Writing**; a **Planned** campaign becomes **Running**. A campaign whose end is before its start is marked: **The end is before the start**.

## Today

- A step comes to Today on its day with **Done**, and to Upcoming before it; it stays until it is done.
- A piece sent for review comes to Today with **Approve**.
- A piece's day comes to Upcoming, and to Today with **Publish**, while it is being written, in review or scheduled.

## Home

- **This week**: what goes out this week, each with its day and channels.
- **In review**: what waits for review, and who sent it.
- **This month**: how many pieces were published this month, channel by channel.
