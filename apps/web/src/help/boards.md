# 📋 Boards

A board is cards in columns: what is waiting, what is being done, what is done. It is for what a project needs; a task list is for what you do today.

## Cards

Press **+** beside the tabs and pick **Board**. Under a column, **Add card** takes a title; press **Enter**.

A card has a key such as `SCR-12`, a description, attachments, subtasks, a priority, labels, a date, who it is given to, and links to notes and tasks. Click a card to open it beside the columns. Under it, its timeline shows its moves, files and comments together, with who made each, a person or an agent.

Click the description to write it; **Ctrl+Enter** (**Cmd+Enter** on a Mac) or **Save** keeps it, **Esc** or **Cancel** leaves it as it was. Files go in **Attachments**: press **Add attachment**, or drop them on the card; a file pasted into the description goes there too. Click a file to open it; its **···** renames it or takes it off the card, and it stays in **Files**. In a comment, **Enter** starts a new line and **Ctrl+Enter** posts it.

Type `@` in its description or a comment to mention someone in the space; with a Scrawl server they are told. See [Working together](working-together).

Drag a card to another column to move it. **Board** and **List** at the top switch between the columns and a table of the same cards.

## Columns

Columns are added, renamed, deleted and dragged into order. Each has a kind, whatever its name:

- **Backlog:** not taken up yet.
- **Active:** being worked on, such as Todo or Doing.
- **Review:** waiting for a person to look; an agent leaves what it finished here.
- **Done** and **Dropped:** finished, or given up.

With a Scrawl server, a column can tell people of a card that comes to it: testers of what reaches Test, say. In the column's **···** menu, **Notify me** turns it on for you; **Notify others…** finds people of the space by name and adds them, and × takes one off. A column that tells someone has a bell beside its name. Whoever moves or adds the card is not told.

## Fields

**Fields**, at the top of the board, adds fields to every card: a number, a date, a choice, a link to a record of a collection, and more. The first link and the first number show on the card.

## Phases

A phase is a goal the cards work toward, one after another, with no dates. The strip over the board shows each phase and how many of its cards are done. When a phase is done, close it: Scrawl asks where its unfinished cards go.

## Templates

A new board starts from one of these; after that, everything can change.

- **Software:** Backlog, Todo, Doing, Review, Done, with phases.
- **Simple:** Todo, Doing, Done.
- **Issue tracking:** Backlog, Todo, In Progress, Done, Canceled and Duplicate; it opens as a list.
- **CRM:** Lead, Contacted, Meeting, Proposal, Won and Lost, each deal with its company, contact and value.

## Filtering

**Search** and **Filter** at the top work as in a task list, and the filter knows the board's fields too: **Estimate is more than 3**.

## Dates and agents

Cards and their subtasks with a date come to Scheduled, under their board's name.

**Copy as brief**, in a card's or a phase's menu, copies it as one text with its linked notes, ready for an agent. An agent connected to Scrawl reads and moves cards itself: see [Agents](ai-agents).
