# 🛒 E-commerce

Selling on marketplaces and your own site: each channel's commission, shipping by a day, returns, and what each order really earns. It sells as [Sales and invoicing](sales) does, and comes with it: its orders and products are Sales'.

## Setting it up

- **Where do you sell?** It starts with the usual ones where you are, such as Amazon, eBay, Etsy and Own site. Each one's commission, its fee per order and when it pays go on its screen.
- **Your carriers:** such as DHL, UPS, FedEx and DPD; **Ship** offers them.
- **Do you keep track of returns?** Yes by default: **Returns**, and **Return** on an order.
- **Write payouts and refunds to Budget?** Asked with Budget and spending; yes by default.

## Orders

It opens on **To ship**: the orders not shipped yet, by the day each must go. Over them: how many are to ship, how many today and how many are late. The channels are chips with their counts; pick one to see only its orders. Each row says when to ship, "Today", "Tomorrow" or "1 day late", its channel, **Order no**, items, total and status, with **Ship**. The other tabs are **Shipped**, **Delivered** and **Cancelled**.

A new order asks its **Channel**, **Order no** and **Buyer** too. One from a channel comes in **Confirmed**, and its **Ship by** day is its day plus the channel's **Ships within (days)**. Orders get **Packed** and **Shipped** besides Sales' statuses.

- **Ship**, on a confirmed or packed order, asks the **Carrier**, the **Tracking no** and the day it was **Shipped on**, and makes it Shipped.
- When an order is set **Delivered**, the day is written, for its payout.
- **Return**, on an order that has gone out, lists its items with how many were sent. Write how many come back and **Why** for each: **Damaged**, **Wrong item**, **Changed mind** or **Other**. Each becomes a return, its refund worked out from the item's price.

An order's window says what it really earns in one line: "₺1.240 · commission ₺248 · shipping ₺60 · cost ₺520 → ₺412 profit · 33%". The commission is the channel's share of the total and its fee per order; the shipping is its **Shipping cost**; the cost is what its products cost. Under it, when the channel pays it: "Payout ₺992 on Nov 12, 14 days after it is delivered". Write **Paid out on** when the money comes.

## Returns

Its tabs are **Waiting** and **Done**. A return has its **Order**, **Product**, **Quantity**, **Why**, **Status**, **Refund** and **Channel**, and is **Requested**, **Received**, **Refunded** or **Rejected**.

- **Received** when it comes back. With Stock, it asks **Back in stock**, ticked; take it off when the item is damaged.
- **Refunded** when the buyer has their money back.

## Channels

Each channel's **Commission**, **Fee per order**, **Ships within (days)**, 2 to start, and **Paid after (days)**; and what it brought: its **Orders**, **Revenue**, **Net profit**, **Returns** and **Return rate**.

A product gets its **Cost**, and counts what it **Sold**, what was **Returned**, its **Return rate** and its **Profit**.

## Today

What is to ship comes to Today on its ship-by day, and to Upcoming before it, with **Ship**. A late one stays under **Overdue** until it is shipped.

## Home

- **Sales by day:** today, yesterday and this month, each with its **Orders**, **Sales**, **Profit** and **Margin**.
- **To ship:** how many, how many today and how many are late.
- **Payouts:** what the channels are still to pay for delivered orders, and when.
- **Returns:** how many are waiting.

## With other apps

- [Sales and invoicing](sales): the same orders, products and customers.
- [Stock and purchasing](stock): a return received goes back into stock, unless **Back in stock** is taken off.
- [Budget and spending](budget): an order set Delivered adds its channel's payout, its total less the commission, as a payment to come. A return refunded adds a payment out. Both go under the **Sales** budget line.
- [Customer support](support): a request's **Channel** can be **marketplace questions** or **reviews** too.
