# 🎯 Goals

Goals for the quarter or the year, and the key results that measure them. For a team or a company that sets goals for a period and checks in on them: each goal shows how far it is, and how far it should be by now.

## Setting it up

- **Goals are set for:** **A quarter**, **Half a year** or **A year**; a quarter unless you pick another. A new goal starts and ends with the period it is set in, and is named by it: "2026 Q4", "2026 H2", "2026".
- **How often are goals checked in?** **Every week**, **Every two weeks**, **Every month** or **Only when something changes**; every week unless you pick another. A goal's check-in comes to its owner's Today on its day.

## Goals

The tabs are **This period**, **Mine**, **Behind** and **Closed**. The screen sums up how much of the period is gone, how many goals there are, how far they are on average, how many are behind or at risk, and how many check-ins are due.

Each row says its owner, the goal it is **Part of**, a bar of its progress with a line where it should be by now, its status, and its last check-in or **Check-in due**.

### A goal

A new one asks its **Name**, **Owner**, you unless you pick another, the goal it is **Part of**, if any, and its **Starts** and **Ends**, filled with the period.

Its window says where it stands: "Behind · 24% · expected 33% by now · 2026 Q4 · ends Dec 31 (in 62 days) · Ahmet Yılmaz". It has its key results, the goals that are part of it, with **+ Goal**, and its check-ins.

A key result is **Measured by** a **Number**, a **Percent**, **Money** or **Done or not**. It has a **Start**, a **Target** and where it is **Now**: "$12,950 of $15,000 · from $11,500". Done or not is 1 when it is done.

### Worked out

- A key result's progress is how far it has come from its start to its target.
- A goal's progress is its key results' on average. With none, it is the average of the goals that are part of it.
- How far it should be by now is the share of its period gone by. A goal short of it is **Behind**; more than a quarter short, **At risk**. Otherwise it is **On track**, and **Done** at 100%.

### Its buttons

- **Check in** asks every key result's value at once, beside what it was, then **How is it going** and **What changed**. How it is going starts as worked out; pick another if you know better, and it stays until the next check-in. The check-in is kept in the goal's **Check-ins**, with what moved, from what to what.
- **Close goal** asks how it ended, **Complete** or **Incomplete**, and **What we learned**. Tick carry over, and the goal starts again in the next period with the key results it did not reach, from where they got to.

## Today

- A goal's check-in comes to its owner's Today with **Check in**, as often as you chose, counted from its start, then from its last check-in. With **Only when something changes**, none comes; press **Check in** when you need it.
- On its last day, a goal comes to its owner's Today with **Close goal**. It shows in Upcoming before.

## Home

**This period** shows how far the goals are on average, how much of the period is gone, and how many are On track, Behind and At risk. **Check-ins due** lists the goals waiting for one. **Closed** counts the goals that ended Complete and Incomplete.

## With other apps

- With [Project management](projects), a project can point at a goal, and a key result can be measured by **Project's progress**, taken from the project's cards.
- With [Product development](product), a Roadmap card can point at a goal, and the goal's window shows those cards.

## Ask an agent

- "Add a goal for this quarter: reach $15,000 a month in revenue, with a key result from $11,500 to $15,000."
- "Check in on the Signups goal: we are at 180."
- "Which goals are behind or at risk?"
- "Close the Q3 goals and carry over what is left."
