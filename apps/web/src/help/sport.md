# 🏃 Sport and training

Your runs, rides, swims and the rest, and a weekly plan whose days come to Today. Each workout says its pace and how close it came to its plan; races keep your aim and your result. The gym's sets are kept in [Gym](gym).

## Setting it up

- **What do you do?** **Running**, **Cycling**, **Swimming**, **Walking**, **Yoga** and **A team sport**; the first four to start. They are the choices of **Sport**, as **Run**, **Cycle**, **Swim**, **Walk**, **Yoga** and **Team sport**, with **Other**. A distance is kept for running, cycling, swimming, walking and other.
- **Keep your races?** Yes by default: **Races**.
- **Keep body measures?** No by default. With yes: **Body**.

## Workouts

Each workout keeps its **Day**, **Time**, **Sport**, **Duration**, **Distance (km)** for a sport with a distance, **Effort (1–10)**, a **Note**, and the **Plan** it was for. Left empty, its name is its plan's, or its sport. One of a plan is **Done** or **Skipped**.

Worked out by itself:

- **Pace**, by the sport: "5:09 /km" running and walking, "2:30 /100 m" swimming, "28.1 km/h" cycling.
- **Plan**, how close it came to its plan: **On plan** within a fifth of the plan's distance, else of its duration; **Short** or **Long** within a half; **Far off** beyond; **Not planned** with no plan.
- Its window says both in a line: "5:09 /km · 17% longer than planned".
- An effort that is not from 1 to 10 is marked.

The list goes week by week, "This week", "Oct 19 – Oct 25", each with how many workouts, how long and how far. Above it are the time this week, and the km this week and this year. Each sport is a chip, to show only its workouts. The tabs are **This week**, **This month** and **All**. A skipped one is not listed.

## Weekly plan

What you plan to do each week: its **Sport**, its **Days**, how long (**Duration**) or how far (**Distance (km)**), **From** when, today to start, and **Until** when, if it ends. Left empty, its name is its sport. Each row says its days, each one "45 min" or "2 h · 55 km", and until when.

**Ongoing** lists the plans going on, **Ended** those past their **Until**.

## Races

Each race: its **Day**, **Sport**, **Distance (km)**, your **Aim** and your **Result**. It opens on a **Calendar** of the races with no result yet. **Coming** lists those ahead, "in 12 days", and **Done** those gone by.

**Write result**, from its day on, asks the **Result** and reminds you of your aim: "Your aim was under 1:50." With no aim, it says what to write: your time, and your place if you like, "1:47:32 · 412th".

## Body

With body measures: each day's **Weight (kg)**, **Waist (cm)** and **Body fat %**. **Since the first (kg)** says how far your weight is from the first one written.

## Today

- Each day of a plan comes to Today, with how long or how far, and to Upcoming before it. **Done** opens the day's workout with the plan's sport, duration and distance: change what was different and save. **Skip** writes it skipped. A day gone by with neither is not kept.
- A race comes to Upcoming before its day, and to Today on it with **Write result**. It stays in Today until its result is written.

## Home

- **This week**: the time and the km this week, and a line for each sport. With none yet, "No workout this week yet."
- **This year**: the km of each sport with a distance.
- **Next race**: how far off the next one is, "in 12 days", and your aim under it.

## With other apps

- [Gym](gym): your gym programs, each time you went, and each move's weight. In one app with this one, its workouts are called **Gym workouts**.
