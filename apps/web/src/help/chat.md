# 💬 Chat

Chat is where a team talks, beside its work: a message can become a task or a card, and a note, a task or a card can be put in a message. History is never deleted, and it all stays on your team's own server.

Chat is there when you are in a shared space on a Scrawl server: see [What a server is](server-about). A space synced with OneDrive talks in its cards' comments instead.

## Rooms and direct messages

**Chat**, under Upcoming in the sidebar, opens in one tab: each space's rooms and your direct messages on the left, the conversation on the right.

- A **room** belongs to a space. A public one is open to everyone in the space, to read and to **Join**; a private one only to its members, who add others. Every space has a **general** room.
- A **direct message** is between you and up to eight others who share a space with you.

**New room**, **Browse rooms** and **New message** are in Chat's **+**. A room's **Info** has its people, its files and its settings.

## Messages

Write in Markdown; a code block is colored and copied with a click. Add images, videos, files and voice recordings. In a message you can:

- **Reply**, quoting it.
- React, **Forward** to up to five conversations, or edit and delete your own.
- Write `@` and a name to mention someone, or `@room` for everyone in it.
- Make it a task or a card, which links back to it.

## Notifications

A direct message and a mention always notify you; a private room every message, a public room only mentions. Each conversation's bell chooses **All**, **Mentions** or **Off**. Turn notifications on for this device in **Settings › Notifications**.

## Search

Chat's search box looks through every conversation, narrowed by **From**, **In**, **Has** and **Date**. **Search** finds messages too, under **Messages**.

## Export

**Export…**, in a conversation's **Info**, saves it as Markdown, one file a month, with its files, in a zip.

## Agents

An agent connected through the server reads the conversations you can, and writes in them as itself. See [Agents](ai-agents).
