# 🏋️ Gym

Your gym programs, written once part by part, and each time you go. Pick the program, and its moves come ticked, each with its weight from the last time: untick what you did not do, change what was different, and save. Each move says how regularly you do it, and your best.

## Setting it up

- **Will you track your program days?** Yes by default: a program has its **Days** and **From**, and its days come to Today and Upcoming; one gone by with no workout stays in Overdue. With no, you pick the program as you add a workout.
- **Will you track each set?** No by default: a move is one line in a workout, 3 × 12 at 45 kg. With yes, each set is a line, with its own reps and weight.

## Workouts

Each time you went, or did not: its **Day**, its **Program**, its **Status**, **Done** or **Didn't go**, and its **Duration**. Left empty, its name is its program's and its day.

Pick its **Program**, and its moves fill in at once, part by part: **Warm-up**, **Workout**, **Cool-down** and **Cardio**. Each comes ticked, with its sets, reps and time from the program, its **Kg** as the last time you did it, and **Last time** beside it: "42.5 kg · 3 × 12". With each set tracked, each set is a line of its own. Untick what you did not do, change what was different, and save. In a workout's window, a move is ticked or unticked in place.

**Moves** says how many moves of its **Workout** part you did: "7 of 8".

Two things are marked: two workouts of one program on the same day, and one you did not go to with moves ticked.

The tabs are **This month**, **This week**, the last seven days, and **Calendar**, colored by status.

## Programs

A program has its **Days** and **From**, when you track program days, its **Duration**, and its notes. Its window lists its items part by part, each with its **Exercise**, **Sets**, **Reps**, **Time**, **Rest** and **Note**. It counts its **Items**, the workouts **Done** with it, and the day of the **Last** one.

It starts with one, **3-Day Full Body**: on Mon, Wed and Fri from the day the app is set up, for 60 minutes. It has a warm-up, eight machine moves, a cool-down of stretches, and 15 minutes of walking or cycling after; its notes say how hard to go, when to add weight, and what to do on days off. Make it yours, or write your own.

With program days, **Program days** shows a program's days on a calendar.

## Exercises

72 moves come ready, the ones most used in a gym, each with its **Muscle group**: **Chest**, **Back**, **Legs**, **Shoulders**, **Arms**, **Core** or **Full body**. Add your own; change or take out any.

Each says, by itself:

- **Best (kg)**, the most you lifted in it, and when it was **Last done**.
- **Last 4 weeks**: in how many of the workouts that had it you did it, "4 of 8", and as a share, **Regular**. Under 7 of 10 is marked.

**Weights** lists the moves with a best. A move's window lists each time you did it, with its sets, reps and kg, the latest first.

## Today

With program days, each one comes to Today, and to Upcoming before it:

- **Start** opens the day's workout, its program picked and its moves filled.
- **Didn't go** writes the day as not gone, with **Undo**.

A day gone by with neither stays in Overdue until you press one.
