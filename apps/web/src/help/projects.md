# 📌 Project management

Your projects, for customers or for yourselves: how each is going, its milestones, the work on it as cards, and if you want the hours spent and its budget. Each project's lead says how it is going with an update every week or two.

## Setting it up

- **Who are your projects for?** **Customers**, **Ourselves** or **Both**, the default. With **Ourselves**, a project has no customer.
- **Will you track time?** No by default. Yes adds **Time**: the hours spent on each project.
- **Each project's milestones:** **Kickoff**, **Planning**, **Delivery** and **Review** to start with. **Start project** adds each to a project, with a day spread from its start to its end.
- **How often does a project lead post an update?** **Every week**, the default, **Every two weeks** or **No updates**. The update comes to Today on its day.
- **Will you keep the budget of your projects?** No by default, and asked only when [Budget and spending](budget) is not chosen too. Yes sets up budget lines and payments, linked to each project.
- **The budget lines every project starts with:** asked with a budget. **Material 40%**, **Labour 40%** and **Transport 10%** to start with; **Start project** adds each, planned at its share of the project's **Contract**.

## Projects

A project has its **Customer**, its **Lead**, its **Start** and **End**, a **Status** and a **Health**: **On track**, **At risk** or **Off track**, set with each update. With a budget it has its **Contract** too. An end before the start is stopped: "The end is before the start".

The tabs are **Active**, **Planned**, **On hold**, **Mine** and **Done**. Over them: how many are active, at risk, late, and missing their update. Each row says by itself:

- **Progress:** how many of its cards are done, "7 of 12", a bar in its health's color.
- **Due:** "Due Dec 15", or "12 days late" once it is past its end.
- **Next milestone:** the next one not done, "Planning · Nov 22".
- **Update:** "No update for 17 days", when one is missing.

Its window starts with a line such as "Active · On track · 7 of 12 cards · due Dec 15 (in 46 days) · next: Planning Nov 22". Beside its fields are its **Updates**, its **Milestones**, its cards on Work and, with time, its hours. Tick a milestone **Done** there; when one is done or its day changes, the project's next milestone follows.

- **Start project**, on a planned one, asks first. It makes the project **Active**, sets its start to today if it has none, and adds its milestones, spread from its start to its end, a week apart when it has no end. With a budget, it adds its budget lines too.
- **Post update** asks its **Health** and **What happened**. How far it got since the last one, whether its end moved and its next milestone are added by itself: "64% done (was 58%) · end moved to Dec 15 · Planning · Nov 22".
- **Track time**, with time, asks **What**, **Hours** and **Day**. Left without hours, a timer starts; **Stop** writes the hours.
- **Close project** asks **How it ended**, **Done** or **Cancelled**; what to do with the **Cards still open**, **Move to Done** or **Drop them**; and a **Last word**, kept as its last update.

## Timeline

Every project but the cancelled ones, a bar from its start to its end. A bar is in its health's color while the project is under way, gray while it is planned, blue once done. Its milestones are diamonds, filled once done; a line marks today, and a late project is hatched red past its end.

Drag a bar to move its start and end together, with **Undo**; press it to open the project. On a computer, **Weeks**, **Months** and **Quarters** set how far it shows, and a bar's end is dragged on its own.

## Work

The work on the projects, a card each, on a board with **Backlog**, **To do**, **In progress**, **Review**, **Done** and **Dropped**. Each card has its **Project**, and a project's progress is the share of its cards that are done. See [Boards](boards).

## Time

With time, **Time** opens on **Week**: a row for each project, a column for each day, with its hours. **All** lists every entry with its project, person, day and hours.

An entry has **What**, **Work** when left empty, its **Person**, **Day** and **Hours**, and **Billable**, ticked to start with, when projects are for customers. Added without hours, a timer starts; **Stop** writes the hours.

## Today

- When an active project's update is due, it comes to Today with **Post update**, saying how it stood last time: "last Nov 3 · On track · 64%". Three days on with none, the project is marked **Update missing**.
- A milestone comes on its day with **Done**, and to Upcoming before.
- A timer running is there with **Stop**.

Late ones stay under **Overdue**.

## Home

- **Projects:** how many are active and at risk, each with its due day.
- **This week:** the milestones due within a week or late, with their project.
- **Work:** its open cards.
- **Hours this week**, with time: the hours, the project with the most (**Most on**) and who put in the most (**Most by**).

## With other apps

- With an app that keeps companies, such as [CRM](crm), a project's **Customer** is one of its companies, and a company's window lists its projects with their status and due day.
- [Budget and spending](budget): a project adds up its budget lines and what was paid in and out for it, and shows a **Budget** bar, "$12,400 of $20,000", yellow from 80% and red over it. Its window lists its budget lines and payments; a payment or an expense can be given its project.
- [Consulting](consulting): with both, the time entries are the ones Consulting keeps.
- [Goals](goals): a key result can be measured by a project's progress.

## Ask an agent

- "Start the Website redesign project."
- "Post an update on Website redesign: at risk, the designer is away until Friday."
- "Which projects are late or missing their update?"
- "Add cards to Work for the Atlas launch: brief, design, copy and review."
- "Close the Mobile app project as done, and drop its cards still open."
