# 🧑‍💼 Recruiting

The jobs you are hiring for, and a card for each person who applied, moved from stage to stage. Their interviews come with it, and what each interviewer thought. For a hiring manager, or a small team that hires.

## Setting it up

- **Your hiring stages:** the columns of the Hiring board, in order. The usual ones are Applied, Screening, Interview, Offer, Hired and Rejected. Hired closes a card as done, Rejected as dropped.
- **Where do candidates come from?** Referral, LinkedIn, Job boards, Website and Agency; the first four are picked. They are the choices of a candidate's **Source**. With none picked, there is no Source.
- **Will you keep a record of interviews?** Yes, unless you say no: it adds Interviews and **Schedule interview**. Under it, **With a score and how it went?**, also yes: feedback then asks a score and an overall verdict.
- **The first steps for someone hired:** Contract signed, Equipment ready, Accounts opened and First week planned. **Hire** adds each to the position with the person's name, due the day they start. Leave the list empty for none.

## Hiring

Each candidate is a card, in the column of their stage. Its face shows their position and source, and their next interview, such as "Technical · Thu 14:00", or **No interview planned**. The board says how many have none planned.

A card keeps their **Position**, which it needs, **Email**, **Phone**, **LinkedIn**, **Source**, **Offer**, **Starts** and **Rejected because**. Put their CV in its description.

Open a card to work on it:

- Press one of its stages to move the card there.
- **Schedule interview** asks the type, **Phone screen**, **Technical**, **Culture** or **Final**, when, and who interviews: you, unless you pick another. The interview is named after its type and the candidate, "Technical · Ayşe Kaya".
- **Make offer**, with an Offer stage, asks the salary and the day they would start, and moves the card to Offer with both.
- **Hire** asks the day they start. It moves the card to Hired and adds the first steps to the position, "Contract signed · Ayşe Kaya", due that day. When nobody is left to hire, the position is **Filled**.
- **Reject** moves the card to Rejected and asks why: **Not a fit**, **Took another offer**, **Withdrew**, **Salary** or **No answer**. A card dragged to Rejected asks too.
- Its interviews are listed day by day, each with a line such as "Mert Yılmaz · 4 · Yes · Clear on trade-offs".

**Schedule interview**, **Make offer** and **Hire** are the position's buttons, so they work while it is **Open**. A board is worked as any other: see [Boards](boards).

## Positions

The jobs you are hiring for. A new one asks its **Title**, **Team**, **Location**, **Type** (**Full-time**, **Part-time**, **Contract** or **Intern**), **Openings**, one unless you say more, and **Hiring manager**, you unless you pick another. It gets a code such as POS-0001, and today as its **Opened** day.

The tabs are **Open**, **On hold**, **Filled** and **Closed**. The screen sums up how many are open, how many people are in the pipeline, and how many offers are out. Each row says:

- its hiring manager;
- how many are at each stage, "5 applied · 1 screening · 1 interview";
- how many are hired of its openings, "0 of 2";
- how many days it has been open.

A position's window shows its candidates' cards. It has **Schedule interview**, **Make offer** and **Hire** too; there they ask which of its candidates.

## Interviews

Every interview on a calendar, in its type's color. An interview keeps its candidate, position, time, type and interviewer, and after it the feedback.

**Write feedback** asks a **Score** from 1 to 5, how it went **Overall**, **Definitely not**, **No**, **Yes** or **Strong yes**, and the **Key take-aways**. The interview is then **Feedback written**. Without scores, it asks only the take-aways.

## Today

- An interview comes to Today at its time with **Write feedback**. It stays there, late, until its feedback is written.
- The first steps that **Hire** added come on the day the person starts.

## Home

**Open positions** shows how many are open and in the pipeline, and each position's stages. With interviews kept, Home also has **Pipeline**, how many candidates are at each open stage; **Interviews**, the ones coming; and **Feedback due**.

## With other apps

With [Time off and team](timeoff) in the same app, **Hire** also adds the person to Employees, with the position's team and the day they start, unless they are there already.

## Ask an agent

- "Add Ayşe Kaya as a candidate for Backend Developer, from LinkedIn, with her CV in the description."
- "Schedule a technical interview with Ayşe Kaya on Thursday at 14:00, with Mert as the interviewer."
- "Whose interview feedback is still due?"
- "Make Ayşe Kaya an offer of 4,200, starting December 1."
- "Hire Ayşe Kaya for Backend Developer, starting December 1."
