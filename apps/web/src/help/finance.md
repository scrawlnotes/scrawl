# 💳 Personal finance

Your money: what comes in and goes out of each account, month by month. You add what you spend with just its amount; what comes again, a salary or the rent, is written by itself on its day; and with a budget, each category shows as a bar.

## Setting it up

- **Your accounts:** **Bank account**, **Credit card** and **Cash** to start. Each becomes an account, its type from its name: one with "card" in it is a **Card**, "cash" **Cash**, "saving" **Savings**, any other a **Bank** account.
- **What comes in and goes out every time the same:** a row each, with its **Name**, **Amount**, **In or out**, **Every**, **Day** of the month and **Category**. It starts with Salary on the 15th, Rent on the 1st, Electricity, Water and Natural gas on the 20th, Internet and Building fee on the 5th, Phone on the 10th and Streaming on the 12th, all with no amount. An amount left empty is asked the first time it comes. Each goes into your first account, next on its day this month, or next month once that day has passed.
- **Keep a monthly budget?** Yes by default: a spending category can have a **Monthly limit**, and shows as a bar.

Electricity, water and gas change each month, so they are never written by themselves: they come to Today with **Paid**. The app keeps money in the currencies you choose as it is set up.

## Transactions

Every bit of money in and out. A new one starts with its **Amount**, then **Out**, **In** or **Transfer**, and its **Category**, picked from chips, the most used first. Its **Account** is the one used last, its **Date** today. **Description** can stay empty: it takes the category's name.

**Transfer** moves money between your accounts, as a card paid from the bank. It asks **To** which account and has no category; its description is "To" and that account's name.

It opens on **This month**, by day, each with its category, account and amount, an amount out as minus.

With more than one currency, a transaction has its **Currency**, its account's unless you change it.

## Accounts

Each account's **Type** (**Bank**, **Card**, **Cash** or **Savings**), its **Opening balance**, what was in it on the day you started, and its **Balance**, worked out: the opening balance with what came in, less what went out, and what was moved to and from it. Its window lists its transactions, the latest first.

## Categories

What you spend on and what comes in: 21 for spending, from **Groceries** and **Rent** to **Pets** and **Other**, and **Salary**, **Freelance** and **Other income**. Each is **Spending** or **Income**, and says what was **Spent this month**; with more than one currency, in the main one. Its window lists its transactions, with their total.

With a budget, give a spending category its **Monthly limit**; left empty, it has no budget. One with a limit says:

- what was spent of it, as a bar;
- what is **Left**;
- its **Status**: **On track**, **Close** from 90% of it, **Over** past it. One over is marked: **Over its limit**.

## Recurring

What comes in and goes out again and again: its **Amount**, **Out** or **In**, how often as **Every** (**Weekly**, **Monthly**, **Every 3 months**, **Every 6 months**, **Yearly**, **Every 2 years**, or **Custom**, which asks **How many** and **Of**: **Days**, **Weeks**, **Months** or **Years**), its **Next** day, **Account** and **Category**. It opens on **By next**, the nearest first.

- **Paid automatically**, on by default, is for what stays the same: a salary, the rent. Once it has an amount, it writes its transaction by itself on its day, or the next time you open the app after it. A message says so, with **Undo**.
- Turn it off for what changes, as electricity. It comes to Today with **Paid**, which asks the **Amount**, the last one to start, and the **Account**.
- One with no amount comes with **Paid** the first time. The amount you give is kept; with **Paid automatically** on, from then on it writes itself.

**Paid** writes the transaction on its **Next** day, today if that is still to come, and moves **Next** on as **Every** says. A new **Amount** is used from the next time on; what was written stays.

## Today

- A recurring one comes to Upcoming before its day and to Today on it, saying how much: "−₺900 · Bank account", "about −₺640 · Bank account" for one that changes, or **No amount yet**.
- One paid automatically writes itself and has no button. The others have **Paid**, and stay in Overdue until they are paid.

## Home

- **Today:** what is due today, or late.
- **Transactions:** this month's in, out and net, and the latest ones with their amounts.
- **Accounts:** each one's balance, and their total.
- **Categories:** with a budget, a bar for each category given a limit. With none, it says to give one a limit.
- **Recurring:** the next ones, each with its amount and day.

## With other apps

- [Shared expenses](split): with **Write your own share in Personal finance?**, yes by default, your own share of a shared expense, as the one ticked **Me**, is written as spent in **Transactions**, from your bank account, with its **Shared expense**. Change the expense and it is written again. The rent written again each month writes your share again too.
