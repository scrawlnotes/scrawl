# 🧱 How apps work

An app is collections, boards and rules set up together for one job: a CRM, invoicing, a gym log. You pick ready templates, answer a few questions, and Scrawl builds it in a folder of its own. Nothing in it is fixed: it is made of the same collections and boards as the rest of Scrawl, and you can change any of it.

## Setting one up

In **Browse** or in a folder, press **+** at the bottom right and pick **App**. Then:

1. Choose one template, or several that work together. Templates chosen together share what they have in common: CRM, Sales and invoicing and Customer support keep the same companies and people.
2. Answer each template's questions, such as who your customers are or which stages a deal goes through.
3. Name the app, choose its folder and, for an app that keeps money, its currencies.

The app comes with an **About** note that says how it was set up and how its parts work together.

## Its page

An app is one row in **Browse**, with its parts under it. Its page has what it has for you today, each part's screens, **About** and **Files**. Press and hold the app's row for **Add to app…** and **Edit app…**.

## Today

An app's work comes to Today with what it takes to do it: a call to log, an invoice falling due, a program day with **Start**, a medicine to take. See [Task lists](task-lists).

## Adding more

**Add to app…** adds another template to an app later. What it shares with what is there is linked, not made twice.

## The templates

**For a business.**
- Customers: CRM, Customer support, Appointments and bookings, Content calendar, Field service, Consulting.
- Money: Sales and invoicing, Budget and spending, Stock and purchasing, E-commerce, Assets and maintenance, Subscriptions and memberships.
- Work: Project management, Product development, Recruiting, Contracts and renewals, Time off and team, Goals, Incidents and actions.

**For a person.**
- Money and home: Personal finance; Home, car and documents; Recipes and meal plan.
- Health: Health, Habits and goals, Gym, Sport and training.
- Life: Travel, Shared expenses, Job search, People and birthdays, Reading and watching.

To change an app once it is set up, see [Editing an app](apps-editing). For one no template covers, see [Building an app with an agent](apps-building).
