# 📨 Job search

Each job you look at and apply for, how far it has gone, its interviews and when to follow up. Move each one on as it goes; the app keeps the days and tells you when it is time to write again.

## Setting it up

- **The stages of an application**, in this order: **Saved**, **Applied**, **Screening**, **Interview**, **Offer**, **Accepted** and **Rejected** to start. Rename, add or take out any. The list says which stage is **waiting**, the first, for a job not applied to yet, and which are **done** and **dropped**: an application at one of those is over.
- **Follow up when you have heard nothing for** **A week**, **Two weeks**, the default, or **Three weeks**.

The app keeps money in the currencies you choose as it is set up.

## Applications

Applications opens on **Active**: those not over, the latest applied first, with how many are at each stage over them: "6 Saved · 7 Applied". Each row says its company, its stage, how long ago it was applied, "12 days", and what is next: "Interview · Thu 14:00", "Follow up today", "Follow up on 12 Nov", or for one not sent yet "1 of 4 done".

A new one asks its position, **Company**, **Location** and **Job post**, its link. **Company** and **Location** offer what you wrote before. Once it is made, it has **Salary**, **Contact** and **Contact email** too. Paste the post in its notes and attach your resume.

Its window has the position as its title, and under it "Linear · Remote · applied 12 days ago" and its stages in a bar: press the stage it has reached. It lists its interviews day by day, and its **To do**, with **Add step**. **Followed up** works once it is sent and until it is over.

Done by itself:

- A new one at **Saved** has its to-dos ready: **Tailor your resume**, **Write a cover letter**, **Find a referral** and **Apply**.
- The day it is applied is written when it leaves **Saved**.
- Each change of stage, and each interview, counts as news.
- An interview moves an application at **Saved**, **Applied** or **Screening** to **Interview**.

### Interviews

Add each interview in the application's window: **When**, with its time, its **Type**, **Phone screen**, **Technical**, **Culture**, **Panel**, **Final** or **Other**, and **With** whom. It is named by itself: "Technical · Software Engineer, Linear". Write how it went in its notes.

In the application's window each is on its day, at its time, with its sign, 📞 💻 🤝 👥 🏁 💬, and "With Ayşe Kaya".

## Today

- An interview comes to Upcoming before its day and to Today on it, at its time, with whom.
- An application sent and not over, with no news for as long as you chose, comes to Upcoming and then to Today as "Follow up · Linear · applied 16 days ago", with **Followed up**. Press it once you have written: it comes again a week later. News starts the count again. Not pressed, it stays in Overdue.
- A to-do given a day comes on that day.

## Home

- **Today:** what comes today: interviews and follow-ups.
- **Applications:** how many are at each stage, those over left out.
