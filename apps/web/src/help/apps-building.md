# 🤖 Building an app with an agent

When no template fits how you work, an agent builds the app with you: the collections, their forms, the links between them, the rules and buttons, and the boards and lists around them. It writes them as Scrawl's own files, the same as a template does, so you change the result as you would any app.

## Before you start

Connect an agent to Scrawl: see [Agents](agents). Then make, or choose, the folder the app goes in.

## Say what you need

Tell it about the work, not about fields: who does what, what is kept, and what you look at each day. For example:

> Make an app for our spare parts business in the Workshop folder. We keep parts with a code, a shelf and a price in TRY and EUR. Customers order parts; an order has lines, and its stock goes out when it is shipped. Warn me in Today when a part runs low. I want to see this week's orders and what they earn.

A few things help:

- Say what each record is called and what it holds, in your own words.
- Say what goes together: an order's lines, a customer's orders.
- Say what should happen by itself, and when: "when an order is shipped".
- Say what you want to see: in Today, as a board, on a calendar.

## What it makes

- **Collections**, each with its fields and its form laid out in columns and sections.
- **Links** between them, and lists of what links to a record, such as a customer's orders.
- **Formulas** and totals: an order's total from its lines, stock from what came in and went out.
- **Rules:** checks that stop a wrong value, buttons such as **Ship**, and what happens when a record is saved.
- **Views**, with filters and calendars, and **boards** or **task lists** where the work moves.
- An **About** note that says how the app works.

Scrawl checks each file the agent writes; a rule with a mistake is refused, with the line, and the agent puts it right.

## Try it, then change it

Add a few records and press its buttons. What is not right, change by hand in **Fields**, or tell the agent, as you would a colleague: "orders also need a delivery address". See [Editing an app](apps-editing).

Everything it changes carries its name, earlier versions stay in **History**, and what it deletes goes to **Trash**.
