# 📋 Boards

A board is cards in columns: what is waiting, what is being done, what is done. It is for what a project needs; a task list is for what you do today.

## Cards

In **Browse** or in a folder, press **+** at the bottom right and pick **Board**. On the board, **+** adds a card to the column on screen.

A board shows one column at a time; swipe, or tap the column names at the top. A card has a key such as `SCR-12`, a description, subtasks, a priority, labels, a date, who it is given to, and links to notes and tasks. Tap a card for its page: its fields, its description, and its timeline of moves and comments, with who made each, a person or an agent.

Press and hold a card for **Status…**, which moves it to another column, **Copy as brief** and **Delete**. **Board** and **List**, beside the board's name, switch between the columns and a list of the same cards.

## Columns

Each column has a kind, whatever its name:

- **Backlog:** not taken up yet.
- **Active:** being worked on, such as Todo or Doing.
- **Review:** waiting for a person to look; an agent leaves what it finished here.
- **Done** and **Dropped:** finished, or given up.

## Fields

A board can add fields to every card: a number, a date, a choice, a link to a record of a collection, and more.

## Phases

A phase is a goal the cards work toward, one after another, with no dates. The phases are over the columns; tap one to see its cards. When a phase is done, close it: Scrawl asks where its unfinished cards go.

## Templates

A new board starts from one of these; after that, everything can change.

- **Software:** Backlog, Todo, Doing, Review, Done, with phases.
- **Simple:** Todo, Doing, Done.
- **Issue tracking:** Backlog, Todo, In Progress, Done, Canceled and Duplicate; it opens as a list.
- **CRM:** Lead, Contacted, Meeting, Proposal, Won and Lost, each deal with its company, contact and value.

## Filtering

The filter button at the top works as in a task list, and knows the board's fields too: **Estimate is more than 3**.

## Dates and agents

Cards and their subtasks with a date come to Today and Upcoming, under their board's name.

**Copy as brief** copies a card as one text with its linked notes, ready for an agent. An agent connected to Scrawl reads and moves cards itself: see [Agents](agents).
