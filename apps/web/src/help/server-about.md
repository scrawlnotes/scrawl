# 🗄️ What a server is

A Scrawl server is a server your team runs on its own machine, with Docker. **You don't need one.** Scrawl is complete without it: on your own, or with others through a shared OneDrive folder.

It is for a team that wants what only a server can do.

## What it adds

- **Changes in seconds:** what one person changes reaches the others in a second or two, not in a minute.
- **Accounts:** each person signs in with an email and a password. People join a space with an invite link.
- **Roles:** the server's **Owner** and **Admins** open spaces and look after the accounts; **Members** see only the spaces they are invited to. In a space, its **Managers** invite and remove people.
- **Chat:** rooms and direct messages beside your work. See [Chat](chat).
- **Agents on the web and your phone:** Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini work on the server's spaces. See [Agents](agents).
- **A personal space on the server**, if you want yours there too: only you see it, not the Owner or the Admins.

## What stays the same

The server keeps the same notes, lists, boards and collections as any other place; your devices still keep their own copies, and work offline. A space can be exported from it as a plain folder, to take it elsewhere whenever you like.

## Who can see what

Everything is on your team's machine; nothing goes through us. Its Owner can reach what is stored on it, so a server someone else runs is theirs to read.

## What it costs

Nothing. The server is open source, and no feature is paid for.

To set one up, see [Setting it up](server-setup).
