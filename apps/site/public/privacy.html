Privacy

Scrawl has no accounts and keeps nothing of yours on servers of ours. What you write stays on your devices and in the places you sync it to. We don't collect it, sell it, use it for ads, or use it to train AI.

Where your notes are

  • On your device. In the web app, in your browser's storage on that device.
  • If you sync them: in a folder of your own OneDrive or Google Drive, or on a Scrawl server. A Scrawl server is run by your team on its own machine, and its owner can reach what is stored on it. We don't run one for you.
  • Each device talks to these directly. Your notes never pass through us.

Microsoft and Google

When you connect OneDrive, Outlook Calendar, Google Drive or Google Calendar, you sign in with Microsoft or Google and allow Scrawl what it asks for. The key from that sign-in stays on the device you signed in on.

  • OneDrive and Google Drive: Scrawl works only in the folder you choose. With Google Drive, it sees only the files Scrawl itself put there.
  • Outlook Calendar and Google Calendar: Scrawl reads your list of calendars and the events of the ones you choose, to show them next to your tasks. It makes one calendar named “Scrawl”, writes there the tasks you choose, and reads back what you change there. It changes no other calendar. Events are read on your device when they are shown; they are not stored, and they are not sent to us.
  • Disconnecting removes the key from the device. You can also take back the permission at any time in your Microsoft or Google account.

Google sign-in on the web

Google gives a web page no lasting sign-in on its own. So in the web app and on a phone, when Scrawl gets or renews its Google key, the request goes through scrawlnotes.com, which adds Scrawl's Google client secret and passes it on to Google. Nothing is stored or logged there, and your files and events never pass through it. The desktop app talks to Google directly.

Google user data

Scrawl uses data from Google only to show it to you in Scrawl, and to keep its “Scrawl” calendar and its Drive folder in step with your tasks and notes. It does not share it with anyone, sell it, use it for ads, or use it to train AI or machine-learning models. It does not give it to AI agents connected to Scrawl. No one at Scrawl can see it.

Scrawl's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI agents

You can connect an AI agent, such as Claude, to Scrawl. The agent reads and writes your notes as you let it, under its own maker's terms. Scrawl itself sends nothing to any AI service.

What we see

  • scrawlnotes.com and app.scrawlnotes.com are served by Cloudflare. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits without cookies and without following you to other sites. We see totals, not people.
  • The desktop app asks GitHub whether a new version is out. GitHub sees that request.
  • Notifications from a Scrawl server reach you through your browser's push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla), encrypted.
  • The apps don't report what you do in them. We don't know who you are.

Scrawl is open source: you can read its code on GitHub.
