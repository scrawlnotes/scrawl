03 · Chat

Talk where the work is.

Rooms and direct messages for each space, next to its notes, tasks and boards.

  • On your own server
    Every message, file and conversation stays on the server you run. Nothing goes through us.
  • To a task, to a card
    A message becomes a task or a card. Notes, tasks and cards sit in messages as links.
  • Mentions reach you
    On your computer and on your phone.
  • Photos, files and voice
    Send them in a message. The history never runs out.
  • Agents in the room
    They read the conversation and write in it, like anyone in the space.

04 · Sync

Synced through your cloud or your own server.

Windows, Mac, the browser and your phone. It works offline, and catches up when you are back.

Alone, or with a few people

Through your OneDrive

Your spaces sync through a folder in your own OneDrive. Share one with a few people; who sees it is up to OneDrive.

PersonalOneDrive · just you
FieldworkOneDrive · 3 people

With a team

On your own server

One Docker container you run.

# compose.yml
image: ghcr.io/scrawlnotes/server
$ docker compose up -d

Only on a server

  • Chat, with notifications on your phone
  • Changes reach everyone in a second or two
  • Accounts and roles
  • Agents on the web: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini
  • Backups

Coming: permissions for files and folders, SSO and two-factor sign-in.

Under the hood

Plain files
Markdown and JSON in a folder. Open them in any editor, put them in git.
No account with us
Sync goes through your OneDrive or your server, never ours.
Merged, not overwritten
Two people change the same note: both changes stay.
Nothing is lost
What sync removes goes to the trash first.
